## High-Level Assessment

### Strongest Categories (Based on the Matrix)
The following categories appear closest to being independently verifiable because they rely on government records, court filings, certified agency records, or documented public datasets:

- **Court eviction filings and sheriff records**
  - UD-101 filing
  - Proof of Service records
  - Sheriff execution documentation
  - Court docket entries

- **Environmental contamination records**
  - County health agency records
  - Historical survey maps
  - Federal court litigation referencing contamination issues

- **Medical records**
  - Hospital records
  - Intake documentation
  - Clinical records
  - Face sheets and treatment records (assuming authenticity is established)

These categories generally provide a better foundation because the records originate from institutions that can authenticate them.

## Areas Requiring Significant Additional Proof

### Financial Embezzlement / Money Laundering
The largest evidentiary gap appears here.
Current exhibits may suggest suspicious patterns, but courts generally require:
- Certified bank records
- Account ownership records
- Beneficial ownership documentation
- FinCEN records
- Wire records
- Tracing analysis by a forensic accountant

For example:
> Consecutive $9,500 withdrawals alone do not necessarily establish structuring.

To prove structuring, investigators commonly seek evidence showing:
- Intent to avoid reporting requirements
- Repeated cash transactions below reporting thresholds
- Knowledge of reporting rules
- A pattern across multiple transactions/accounts

---

### Identity Theft / Cyber Intrusion
Several claims currently appear dependent upon:
- ISP records
- Meta logs
- Carrier records
- Device correlation evidence

Without subpoenaed provider records, it may be difficult to attribute activity to a specific person.

To establish attribution, investigators commonly seek:
- Subscriber records
- Login histories
- Device fingerprints
- SIM-change logs
- IP allocation data
- Platform preservation records
