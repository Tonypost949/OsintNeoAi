# What the CIA packet establishes — and what it does not
Written 2026-11-10 by Neo, from the documents in hand only (25 CIA Reading Room releases + 100 sequence pulls, all byte-verified against the Internet Archive mirror). Graded per claim.

## What is DOCUMENTED
1. **A SECRET special-access program — CENTER LANE, run by Army intelligence (INSCOM) — took a second contractor in 1984, and the contractor it took was McDonnell Douglas Astronautics Company at its Huntington Beach plant, 5301 Bolsa Ave.** (Training Programs memo, Mar 1, 1984; chronology, CIA-RDP96-00788R001100020001-8 p.6, May 9, 1984: "Dialogue initiated with McDonnell Douglas Astronautics Company, Huntington Beach, CA to comply with directive to obtain second contractor.")
2. **The purpose was evaluating people, not hardware.** Buzby's Request to Contract Services (CIA-RDP96-00789R003000020001-6, Jun 22, 1984): MDAC-HB engaged sole-source to evaluate up to two individuals under a project "identified as and entitled 'Project Star,'" ~Aug 1–Nov 30, 1984, expressly under human-subjects rules (Procedure 13, DoD 5240.1-R; 45 CFR 46).
3. **The first month of testing was sited at Huntington Beach.** The Statement of Work (CIA-RDP96-00789R003000020016-0) lists, for month 1 at HB: computer tests, "Human Reading," "Psychokineses Party" [sic], Remote PK, Acoustical Detection, Medical Evaluation. Month 2 elsewhere (Princeton/Menlo Park/Austin/Syracuse; San Francisco; Washington DC). Task III: recommendations for use "in relation to intelligence and counterintelligence operations."
4. **MDAC came in on recommendation from inside the intelligence community, and its people were read in.** Trip report (CIA-RDP96-00789R003000020010-6, May 11/14, 1984): MDAC "recommended by CIA/DIA personnel familiar with CENTER LANE"; attendees executed security indoctrination statements and received the Level 3 briefing; an unnamed man "handles classified contracts for MDAC."
5. **MDAC's own psi research predated the contract action.** May 4, 1984 position paper cites "valid scientific replications" by "McDonnell Douglas Corporation" alongside Princeton, Bell Labs and SRI.
6. **Named MDAC-Huntington Beach people in the packet:** Dr. Robert (Bob) Wood; Jack Houck (his PK-party rod was analyzed by the MDAC Metallurgical Laboratory); Col. John Alexander; John Schuessler; J. E. Prentice; George A. Ursini; Donald Keith Banks; Edward John Pfarr Jr.

## Why that matters, stated only as far as the paper goes
- This is not a rumor layer: it is the government's own paper trail — requirement, recommendation, security indoctrination, a sole-source contract request, and a statement of work — placing a classified human-evaluation program inside a working aerospace plant in Huntington Beach in 1984, with named plant personnel.
- The program's own framing was operational, not academic: the SOW's final task is recommendations for intelligence and counterintelligence use.
- The chain is dated and continuous: SRI was the incumbent; MDAC was brought in as the alternate/second contractor, with the Army's intelligence chief (MG Odom) on record: "…that we try for two contractors."

## What is NOT documented (gaps, kept separate)
- No signed contract, award, or results are in the packet — requirement + request + SOW only. Whether the testing happened as scoped is NOT YET DOCUMENTED here.
- The two individuals "Project Star" was to evaluate are not named anywhere in hand.
- The trip report's Inclosures 2 & 3 (MDAC biographies) are not in the document; the sequence position that would hold them (CIA-RDP96-00789R003000020012) is NOT FOUND on the mirror.
- **Separation held:** the packet's wells (a SRI SQUID magnetometer well; the 1969 Santa Barbara blowout) are NOT the Huntington Beach shelter-site wells, and no document in the packet connects MDAC's 1984 program to the Beach/Cameron parcels' 2020 sale. Those are separate tracks and stay separate until a document joins them.
