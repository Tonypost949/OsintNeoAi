# Dossier Attribution Purity Rule
## Rule: Names Only When First Officially On Paper

A name — individual, entity, trust, company, or organization — may only appear in a dossier starting from the exact point it first appears on an official document or official narrative record (deed, plat, permit, newspaper, military record, court filing, assessment roll, etc.).

It may NOT appear before that first official citation date, regardless of speculation, oral history, or assumption.

### Example:
- Yamada first appears in the official Orange County Assessment Rolls circa 1962/1963.
- This dossier covers pre-1960 strictly.
- Therefore Yamada does not appear anywhere in this dossier.
- If a post-1960 addendum is produced, Yamada enters at the 1962 assessment entry — and not one day before.

This rule applies to all future dossier and archival investigation work.
