## 1. Executive Pitch & Mission Statement
**OsintNeoAi** is an advanced autonomous open-source intelligence (OSINT), legal forensic analytics, and regulatory compliance platform. Built upon the proprietary **Trinity Verification Engine** (Physical Reality Constraints -> Stochastic Likelihood -> Baseline Frequency & Mean-Reversion), OsintNeoAi transforms petabyte-scale public records, judicial filings, corporate registries, and geospatial municipal datasets into verified, tamper-proof evidentiary graphs.

By pairing state-of-the-art multimodal AI with rigorous chain-of-custody cryptographic hashing (SHA-256), OsintNeoAi automates the investigative analysis required for public accountability, municipal integrity, civil rights enforcement, and cross-jurisdictional transparency.

## 2. Technical Architecture & Google Cloud Integration
OsintNeoAi leverages the full Google Cloud ecosystem to process and cross-reference authoritative data:
* **Vertex AI & Gemini 1.5/2.0 Multimodal API:** Deep document extraction and contextual synthesis across unstructured municipal agendas, stamped court dockets, land use permits, and corporate disclosures.
* **BigQuery & BigQuery Property Graphs (GQL):** Multi-hop entity resolution and topological mapping connecting corporate entities, registered agents, municipal contracts, and property transactions across decades.
* **Cloud Storage & Dynamic Tiering:** Immutable evidence vaults maintaining primary source documents, Bates-stamped exhibits, and cryptographic audit ledgers.
* **Google Cloud Run & Cloud Functions:** Serverless real-time scrapers and automated regulatory docket monitoring.
