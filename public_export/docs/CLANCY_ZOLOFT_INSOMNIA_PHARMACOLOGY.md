### II. WHY ZOLOFT CAUSES IMMEDIATE SLEEPLESSNESS (THE PHARMACOLOGY)

Unlike most other SSRIs, **Sertraline (Zoloft)** has unique pharmacological properties that make it notoriously activating:

1. **Dopamine Transporter (DAT) Inhibition:**
   * In addition to inhibiting serotonin reuptake, Sertraline possesses weak but clinically significant **dopamine reuptake inhibition**. In postpartum women with hypersensitive dopamine receptors, this produces an amphetamine-like stimulating effect, making sleep impossible.
2. **5-HT2A Receptor Stimulation:**
   * The sudden flood of synaptic serotonin stimulates 5-HT2A receptors in the brainstem, which directly suppresses deep non-REM restorative sleep and fragments sleep continuity.
3. **Early Akathisia Masked as Insomnia:**
   * The patient experiences an agonizing internal restlessness and somatic tension in their legs and chest when lying in bed, misidentified by clinicians as simple "anxiety."
