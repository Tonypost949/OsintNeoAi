# WWJD (Ethical & Fiduciary Alignment) Matrix

## 1. Core Maxims
* **The Ultimate Authority:** Fiduciary and statutory obligations transcend informal digital arrangements. Real estate transactions, HOA management, and habitability grievances must conform to California statutory disclosure requirements.
* **The Fiat Contradiction:** Operating through informal consumer communication layers (e.g. personal email accounts) creates an artificial barrier to transparency when negotiating regulated contracts (such as rent-to-own agreements) without statutory Transfer Disclosure Statements (TDS).
* **Impossibility of Deception:** Deletion or concealment in personal digital channels does not erase physical site conditions, municipal code records, or statutory liability.
* **Messenger Transparency:** All digital communication nodes must be audited and classified according to compliance standards.

## 2. Investigative Directives
1. **Preserve Raw Digital Artifacts:** Ensure `.eml`/`.msg` exports contain intact MIME structures, SPF/DKIM authentication, and `Received:` routing headers.
2. **Statutory Delivery:** Transition all formal notices from informal messaging to USPS Certified Mail with Return Receipt Requested.
3. **Multi-Node Pattern Analysis:** Cross-reference correspondence across related communication nodes to document coordinated evasion of disclosures.
