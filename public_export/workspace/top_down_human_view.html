👔 Top-Down Human Accountability Routing

No matter the electronic form or web portal, public record communications cascade from top-level agency directors down to designated records officers.

Target Hierarchy: County Housing & Development Department

Level 1 - Executive Leadership

Executive Director / Agency Head

Receives formal courtesy copy & legal compliance notice
Level 2 - Statutory Officer

General Counsel / Designated FOIA Officer

Primary addressee for statutory deadline & record production
Level 3 - Operations Clerk

Public Records Custodian / Department Clerk

Extracts physical records & uploads digital response files
