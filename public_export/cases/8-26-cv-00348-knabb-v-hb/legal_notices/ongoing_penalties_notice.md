# Formal Notice of Ongoing RCRA Statutory Violations & Daily Civil Penalty Accrual
**Case:** *Knabb v. City of Huntington Beach*  
**Docket:** 8:26-cv-00348-JWH-ADS (C.D. Cal.)

---

**TO:** City of Huntington Beach and Counsel of Record

### 1. Statutory Authority
Pursuant to 42 U.S.C. § 6972(a) and the Federal Civil Penalties Inflation Adjustment Act (implemented at 40 C.F.R. Part 19), violations of RCRA statutory provisions and orders carry statutory civil penalties of up to **$70,117 per day, per violation**.

### 2. Statement of Continuing Non-Compliance
Notice is hereby given that the environmental conditions and statutory violations set forth in the Complaint remain un-remediated. Each 24-hour period of continued unpermitted storage, disposal, or failure to abate an imminent and substantial endangerment constitutes a separate, distinct violation under federal law.

### 3. Reservation of Rights
Plaintiff reserves the right to seek the maximum statutory penalty permitted by law for each cumulative day of non-compliance through the date of final judgment or decree.
