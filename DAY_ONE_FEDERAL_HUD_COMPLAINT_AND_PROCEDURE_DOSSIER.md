## 1. DAY-ONE HUD COMPLAINT FILING PROCESS (FHEO INTAKE)

### Step 1: Submitting HUD Form 903.1 (Online / Phone / Mail)
1. **Online Filing Portal:** Access the official HUD Office of Fair Housing and Equal Opportunity (FHEO) complaint form directly at https://www.hud.gov/fairhousing/fileacomplaint
2. **Telephone Intake:** Call HUD FHEO National Hotline at `1-800-669-9777` (TTY: `1-800-927-9275`).
3. **Mail/Email Submission:** Direct written Form 903.1 to Region IX FHEO Office (San Francisco / California Jurisdiction).

### Step 2: FHEO Jurisdictional Review & Intake Interview (Days 1–10)
- HUD FHEO reviews the complaint for threshold jurisdiction:
  1. Did the alleged unlawful practice occur within 1 year of filing (42 U.S.C. § 3610(a))? *(Note: Under the Continuing Violation Doctrine, ongoing retaliatory acts extend the 1-year window back across the entire 2021–2026 timeline).*
  2. Does the respondent control covered housing or receive federal financial assistance under Section 504?
  3. Does the claim cite protected classes (Disability & Retaliation)?

### Step 3: Formal Complaint Service & Answer (Days 10–30)
- HUD serves formal notice and copy of complaint upon Respondent(s).
- Respondent is required by federal law to file an Answer within 10 days of receipt.

### Step 4: Investigation & Conciliation (Days 30–100)
- HUD investigators issue subpoenas, inspect physical property, take sworn statements, and audit records.
- HUD is statutorily obligated to offer voluntary Conciliation (settlement negotiation) throughout the investigation.

### Step 5: Cause Determination & Charge of Discrimination (Day 100)
- **If Reasonable Cause Exists:** HUD issues a Charge of Discrimination.
- The case is set for hearing before a Federal HUD Administrative Law Judge (ALJ) or elected to be tried in U.S. District Court (represented by the U.S. Department of Justice Civil Rights Division).

## 3. HOMELESS COURT WARRANT CLEARANCE & ADVOCACY FRAMEWORK

1. **Homeless Court Program (HCP) Purpose:** Specially structured diversion courts designed to resolve outstanding non-violent warrants, citations, and misdemeanor cases stemming directly from homelessness, housing deprivation, and retaliatory law enforcement interactions.
2. **Requirement for Diversion:** Participation in local homeless services, disability advocacy programs, or HUD/legal aid intake satisfies court resolution criteria.
3. **Warrant Recall Process:** Upon enrollment in Homeless Court intake through a recognized local service provider or legal aid attorney, open warrants are recalled and cleared without incarceration, allowing unhindered pursuit of HUD remedies and housing stability.
