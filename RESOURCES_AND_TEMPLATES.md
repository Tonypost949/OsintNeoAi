## 1. Property Ownership Verification (1133 N Formosa Ave)
*Key Fact: Los Angeles County restricts online owner-name searches for privacy reasons. To obtain ownership information, you must contact the offices directly.*

### Option 1: Contact the Assessor's Office
Email `helpdesk@assessor.lacounty.gov` with the APN **5531-007-059** and the property address.
- **Phone:** (213) 974-3211
- **Email for Data Sales:** `datasales@assessor.lacounty.gov`

### Option 2: Contact the Registrar-Recorder/County Clerk
Real estate records are filed by grantor name, grantee name, and year—not by address in their public system.
- **Email:** `recorder@rrcc.lacounty.gov`
- **Phone (Real Estate Records):** (562) 462-2133
- **Phone (Recording):** (800) 201-8999, option 2
- **Address:** 12400 Imperial Highway, Norwalk, CA

## 3. LA County Superior Court Cases
Search by party name or case number at [lacourt.org](https://www.lacourt.org) under "Online Services" → "Case Information."

### Known Case Numbers to Search:
- **BC376391** – Citibank v. Andrew C Falk (filed 2007)
- **BC392301** – Andrew Falk v. Rozita Sameyah (filed 2008)

### In-Person Requests
Some records require visiting the courthouse where the case was filed. The **Stanley Mosk Courthouse** (111 N Hill St, Los Angeles) handles many civil cases.

## 4. Federal Court Cases (PACER)
*Note: PACER requires a registered account and charges $0.10 per page (capped at $3.00 per document).*

### Steps:
1. Register at [pacer.uscourts.gov](https://pacer.uscourts.gov).
2. Search the PACER Case Locator for a nationwide index of federal cases.
3. Search by party name: `"Andrew Falk"` and `"15 Swift LLC"`.

## 5. California Business Entity Records
Search the California Secretary of State bizfile portal at [bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov](https://bizfileonline.sos.ca.gov).

### Search Tips:
- Search `"15 SWIFT LLC"` — note the space (not `"15SWIFT"`).
- Search `"Andrew Falk"` for any business affiliations.
- Search `"Normandie"` for entities registered at that address.
*Note: For certified copies, you may need to submit a written request with fees.*
