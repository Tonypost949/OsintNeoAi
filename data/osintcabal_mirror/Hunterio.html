Hunter.io - Email Finder

This tool redirects you to Hunter.io and extracts emails from domain and name queries. Make a free account with a gmail burner, log in, then run this tool when needed!

Domain Search: Find all email addresses associated with a company domain.

Example: Microsoft.com

Name Search: Find email addresses for a specific person at a company.

Example: First Name: John, Last Name: Smith, Domain: microsoft.com
