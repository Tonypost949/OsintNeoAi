## 4. Exhaustive Submitter Eligibility Analysis Under Statutory Frameworks

To evaluate the legal standing and eligibility of Anthony Michael DiMarcello III as a whistleblower and *qui tam* relator, four statutory frameworks must be applied to the timestamped factual record:

### 4.1 Federal False Claims Act (31 U.S.C. § 3730(e)(4)(B))

The False Claims Act (FCA) bars *qui tam* actions based upon public disclosures unless the relator qualifies as an **"original source"**:

$$\text{Original Source Standing} = \text{Prong 1 (Pre-Disclosure Voluntary Transmission)} \quad \lor \quad \text{Prong 2 (Post-Disclosure Material Addition)}$$

#### A. Prong 1 Eligibility: Voluntary Pre-Disclosure Reporting (31 U.S.C. § 3730(e)(4)(B)(i))
- **Statutory Text:** An individual qualifies as an original source if, *"prior to a public disclosure under subsection (e)(4)(a), [they] voluntarily disclosed to the Government the information on which the allegations or transactions are based."*
- **Application to Relator:** 
  1. *Timing:* DiMarcello transmitted his initial dossier connecting Shea Homes and the Angel Stadium transactions on **May 18, 2022**, and his multi-agency disclosure on **May 22, 2022**. While the state court ex parte motion occurred on May 16, the federal search warrant affidavit was **not publicly disseminated across nationwide news media or federal district court dockets until May 24, 2022**.
  2. *Automatic Exemption:* For any information provided prior to the public disclosure of that specific allegation, Prong 1 confers **automatic original source status**. The relator is not required to prove that their knowledge "materially adds" to what the government already knew.
