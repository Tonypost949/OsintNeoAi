Sep 15, 2026
These campaign documents outline a legal and housing crisis involving
Dr. Ann Verma
, a child psychiatrist facing
eviction and hazardous living conditions
in West Hollywood. Despite professional reports documenting
toxic mold, active termite infestations, and structural instability
, the landlord allegedly performed unpermitted construction that left the residence in a state of disrepair. The materials detail a
complex timeline of failed interventions
by city and state agencies, alongside aggressive enforcement actions involving sheriff’s deputies. Volunteers are being organized to support Dr. Verma as she prepares for a
September 28 jury trial
and a separate fraud lawsuit against her landlord. The provided information serves as a
press and outreach kit
, offering verified evidence to secure legal representation and media coverage. This coordinated effort seeks to highlight
tenant harassment
and the failure of local officials to issue a stop-work order despite safety warnings from a licensed engineer.
