### III. THE GREY-MARKET DIVERSION VECTOR (*HI-TECH PHARMA* PRECEDENT)

As established in federal criminal case law (*United States v. Jared Wheat / Hi-Tech Pharmaceuticals*, N.D. Ga.):

1. **Secondary Wholesaler Infiltration:**
   * When major retail pharmacy chains experience localized stock shortages, they frequently turn to **secondary wholesale jobbers**.
   * Secondary jobbers often source surplus inventory from repackagers where counterfeit generic pills (manufactured with identical dies and packaging) are commingled with authentic lots.
2. **The Whitman Lab Proximity:**
   * The **Andrew Billings Whitman Lab** (122 Commercial St, Whitman, MA) was actively pressing millions of counterfeit tablets less than **15 miles from the Kingston and Plymouth CVS pharmacies** where Clancy prescriptions were filled.
3. **Absence of Lot Testing by Pharmacies:**
   * Retail pharmacies do not perform gas chromatography-mass spectrometry (GC-MS) on delivered stock. They rely exclusively on visual inspection and DSCSA barcodes, which are easily cloned on commercial blister packs.
