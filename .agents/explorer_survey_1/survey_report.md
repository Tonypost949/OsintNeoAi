### CASE 1: UNITED STATES v. HARISH "HARRY" SIDHU
* **Official Court Caption:** *United States of America v. Harish "Harry" Sidhu*
* **Court Jurisdiction:** United States District Court for the Central District of California (Southern Division — Santa Ana)
* **Docket Number:** `8:23-cr-00108-CJC` *(Related Search Warrant Docket: `8:22-mj-00185`)*
* **Assigned Judicial Officer:** Hon. Cormac J. Carney, United States District Judge
* **Defendant:** Harish "Harry" Sidhu (Former Mayor of the City of Anaheim, California)
* **Filing / Plea Date:** August 16, 2023 (Plea Agreement under Fed. R. Crim. P. 11)
* **Maximum Statutory Exposure:** 54 Years Federal Imprisonment

#### Statutory Citations & Charges
1. **Count 1: Wire Fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343)**
   * *Charge:* Scheme to defraud the City of Anaheim, its City Council, and its citizens of confidential, proprietary commercial negotiating information and honest services.
   * *Overt Act:* Transmitting confidential internal municipal property appraisals and closed-session negotiating parameters via electronic mail and text messaging to an Anaheim Angels baseball team representative/consultant in exchange for an anticipated $1,000,000 political campaign contribution.
2. **Count 2: Obstruction of Justice (18 U.S.C. § 1519)**
   * *Charge:* Destruction, alteration, and falsification of records in federal investigations.
   * *Overt Act:* Knowingly altering, destroying, mutilating, and deleting electronic communications, emails, and text messages from personal and official devices with the intent to impede, obstruct, and influence an active FBI and federal grand jury investigation into the Angel Stadium sale.
3. **Count 3: False Statements to Federal Law Enforcement (18 U.S.C. § 1001(a)(2))**
   * *Charge:* Making materially false, fictitious, and fraudulent statements to an Executive Branch agency.
   * *Overt Act:* During a voluntary interview with FBI Special Agents on September 16, 2022, Sidhu falsely stated that he never provided confidential city negotiation information, internal pricing parameters, or appraisal documents to Angels representatives, and falsely denied expecting financial campaign support from Angels affiliates.
4. **Count 4: False Statements to the Federal Aviation Administration (18 U.S.C. § 1001(a)(2))**
   * *Charge:* Materially false statements regarding aircraft registration.
   * *Overt Act:* Providing false purchase documentation and an out-of-state Arizona address to the FAA during the registration of a helicopter (Robinson R44) to evade approximately $15,887.50 in California state sales and use taxes.

#### FBI Search Warrant Affidavit Findings (SA Brian Adkins — Case No. 8:22-mj-00185)
* **Unsealing Date:** May 16, 2022 (36-page sworn affidavit).
* **The $1,000,000 Quid Pro Quo Intercept:**

    > *"I am going to ask him for $1 million... I'll say, 'You know what? I'm going to need $1 million to get reelected... I have to raise it.'... We'll have to get it from Angels people."*
* **Mock City Council Rehearsals:**
  * Wiretaps and electronic surveillance established that Sidhu, Ament, and political consultant Jeff Flint (FSB Core Strategies) conducted scripted "mock council meetings" prior to official public hearings to choreograph council questioning, prepare defensive talking points, and guarantee the passage of the $320M stadium sale.
* **Leaked Confidential Land Appraisal:**

* **Grand Jury Witness Tampering:**

#### Verbatim Affidavit Findings & Factual Narrative (FBI SA Bradley H. Zartman)

   * A credible and reliable Government Source revealed that dating back to 2017, the Source purchased methamphetamine from Ryan (Ryan in California, Source in New Jersey). Ryan and the Source negotiated volume and price over cellular phone using coded language.

     * *Ryan Reply:* `"Best seats are in the 6100_6200 section"`
     * *Meaning:* 1 pound of methamphetamine priced between **$6,100 and $6,200**.
3. **Controlled Payment Operation:**
   * On **May 6, 2019**, the Source sent **$3,000** in government-furnished cash via USPS Priority Mail to the Huntington Beach, CA residence of Ryan's friend, as instructed by Ryan.
   * On **May 7, 2019**, Ryan confirmed receipt of the tracking message via telephone.
   * On **May 8, 2019**, the cash parcel was delivered to the Huntington Beach residence.
4. **Controlled Delivery & Interception:**
   * On **May 20, 2019**, at Ryan's direction, a courier mailed approx. 1 pound of methamphetamine from Long Beach, CA to a law enforcement-controlled post office box in Trenton, NJ.
   * On **May 23, 2019**, federal agents intercepted and seized the parcel.
5. **DEA Laboratory Chemical Confirmation:**
   * The U.S. Department of Justice, Drug Enforcement Administration (DEA) Northeast Laboratory chemically tested and confirmed the parcel contained **435 grams of methamphetamine** (approx. 0.96 lbs).
6. **Confession & Post-Miranda Admissions (November 20, 2019):**
   * FBI SA Bradley H. Zartman and agents interviewed Ryan at his residence in **Sunset Beach, California**.
   * Ryan admitted agreeing in April/May 2019 to sell 1 lb methamphetamine for ~$6,000.
   * Ryan admitted directing the $3,000 cash to his friend's Huntington Beach address, using the cash to buy 1 lb methamphetamine from a local supplier, and enlisting another friend to ship the parcel from Long Beach to Trenton.
   * Ryan admitted using friends and third parties specifically to insulate himself from criminal liability.
   * Ryan admitted to multiple prior 1-to-2 pound methamphetamine shipments to the Government Source.
