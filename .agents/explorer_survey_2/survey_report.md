## 2. PILLAR 1: CALIFORNIA HCD OFFICIAL NOTICE OF VIOLATION (DECEMBER 8, 2021)

### A. Statutory & Legal Authority
* **Primary Statute:** California Surplus Land Act (SLA), codified at **California Government Code §§ 54220–54234**.
* **Enabling Reform Legislation:** Assembly Bill 1486 (Ting, Stats. 2019, ch. 664) and Senate Bill 79 (Stats. 2019, ch. 663), effective January 1, 2020.
* **Administrative Oversight Authority:** California Government Code **§ 54230.5** and **§ 65585.1** (granting HCD direct enforcement, review, and civil penalty authority).

### B. Issuing Officers & Key Personnel
* **Issuing Agency:** State of California Department of Housing and Community Development (HCD) — Division of Housing Policy Development / Housing Accountability Unit (HAU).
* **Signatory Officer:** **Megan Kirkeby**, Deputy Director, Division of Housing Policy Development.
* **Department Director:** **Gustavo Velasquez**, Director, California Department of Housing and Community Development.
* **Recipient Entity:** City of Anaheim / Anaheim City Council (Attn: Harry Sidhu, Mayor; Jim Vanderpool, City Manager; Robert Fabela, City Attorney).

### C. Subject Property Description
* **Property Location:** Angel Stadium of Anaheim & surrounding municipal acreage, situated at **2000 East Gene Autry Way** and **2200 East Katella Avenue**, Anaheim, CA 92806 (Orange County APNs: 232-011-34, 232-011-35, 232-011-36, 232-011-37, and related Platinum Triangle parcels).
* **Acreage:** Approximately **150 to 151.1 acres** of publicly owned, contiguous real property.

### D. Findings of Fact & Verbatim Statutory Violations
1. **Failure to Declare Land Surplus (Cal. Gov. Code § 54221(b)(1)):**
   * *Statutory Requirement:* Prior to taking any action to dispose of real property, the legislative body of a local agency must take formal action in a regular public meeting declaring the land either "surplus land" or "exempt surplus land."
   * *HCD Finding:* The City of Anaheim failed to hold a public hearing or adopt a formal resolution declaring the Angel Stadium property surplus or exempt surplus before entering into direct disposition negotiations with SRB Management Co. LLC.
2. **Failure to Issue Written Notice of Availability (Cal. Gov. Code § 54222):**
   * *Statutory Requirement:* A local agency disposing of surplus land MUST send a written Notice of Availability (NOA) via certified mail or electronic delivery to:
     * (a) Local public entities, housing sponsors certified by HCD, and developers for affordable housing purposes (§ 54222(a));
     * (b) Park districts and regional open space agencies (§ 54222(b));
     * (c) School districts (§ 54222(c)).
   * *HCD Finding:* The City of Anaheim completely bypassed the statutory notification requirements, engaging in closed, exclusive bilateral negotiations with SRB Management Co. LLC without providing affordable housing entities any opportunity to submit proposals.
3. **Failure to Comply with 60-Day Notice & 90-Day Good Faith Negotiation Mandates (Cal. Gov. Code § 54223):**
   * *Statutory Requirement:* Entities receiving an NOA have 60 days to express interest. If an entity expresses interest, the local agency must enter into good-faith negotiations for a minimum of 90 days.
   * *HCD Finding:* Anaheim precluded all qualified housing developers from the statutory negotiation window by privately executing the December 2019 Disposition and Development Agreement (DDA).
4. **Rejection of Grandfathering Exemption Defense (Cal. Gov. Code § 54234):**
   * *Anaheim Defense:* The City argued that the transaction was exempt from AB 1486 under § 54234 because the City had pre-existing lease and negotiation rights with the Angels dating back to the 1996 stadium lease.
   * *HCD Determination:* HCD unequivocally rejected this defense, determining that the 2019 agreement with SRB Management Co. LLC constituted an entirely new purchase and sale contract with a new entity, far exceeding the scope of the 1996 lease, and therefore fully subject to the amended Surplus Land Act.

### E. Penalty Computation & 60-Day Cure Mandate
* **Statutory Penalty Framework (Cal. Gov. Code § 54230.5(a)(1)):**
  Under § 54230.5(a)(1), a local agency that disposes of land in violation of the Surplus Land Act is subject to a mandatory statutory civil penalty equal to **30 percent of the final sales price of the land** for a first violation (escalating to 50 percent for subsequent violations).
* **Mathematical Calculation:**
  $$\text{Gross Contract Value} = \$320,000,000.00$$
  $$\text{Statutory Civil Penalty Rate} = 30\%$$
  $$\mathbf{\text{Mandatory Penalty Amount}} = \$320,000,000.00 \times 0.30 = \mathbf{\$96,000,000.00}$$
* **60-Day Statutory Cure Period:**
  HCD gave the City of Anaheim **60 days** from the December 8, 2021 issuance date to cure the violation by either:
  1. Voiding the unlawful agreement and restarting the disposition process in full compliance with the Surplus Land Act; or
  2. Committing the full $96,000,000 statutory penalty to off-site affordable housing creation.

### F. Enforcement Litigation & Settlement Suspension
* **Superior Court Action:** *State of California ex rel. Department of Housing and Community Development v. City of Anaheim*, Orange County Superior Court Case No. `30-2020-01131102-CU-MC-CJC`.
* **Stipulated Judgment (April 2022):** California Attorney General Rob Bonta negotiated a stipulated settlement wherein Anaheim agreed to pay $96,000,000 into a local affordable housing trust fund ($45M offsite housing fund + $51M dedicated credit) while allowing the sale to proceed.
* **Emergency AG Stay (May 16, 2022):** Upon receiving briefings from the FBI regarding active wiretap intercepts proving Mayor Sidhu’s bribery solicitation and honest services fraud, Attorney General Bonta filed an emergency motion requesting an immediate 60-day freeze on the settlement, leading directly to the termination of the deal.
