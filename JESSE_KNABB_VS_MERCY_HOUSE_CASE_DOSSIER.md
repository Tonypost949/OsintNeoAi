## 2. Core Legal Allegations & Causes of Action

1. **Toxic Exposure & Hazardous Dust Discharge:**
   - Exposure to Hexavalent Chromium ($Cr^{VI}$) at **49x statutory safety limits** at 17631 Cameron Ln & 17642 Beach Blvd site.
   - High-pressure washing mobilized aerosolized heavy metals and contaminated runoff onto residents.
2. **Public Fund Misuse & False Claims Act Violations:**
   - Misallocation of CARES Act, ARPA, and HUD CoC public pass-through grants.
   - Self-dealing transactions between CHDO non-profit entities and private real estate partnerships.
3. **Whistleblower Retaliation & Civil Rights Deprivation:**
   - Unlawful eviction threats, career disruption, and retaliation following formal regulatory disclosures.
