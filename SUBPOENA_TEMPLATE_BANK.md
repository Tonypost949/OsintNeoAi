**TO:** [Bank/Financial Institution Name]

## DEFINITIONS & INSTRUCTIONS
1. **"Record"** means any and all statements, ledgers, wire transfers, signature cards, and KYC documents associated with the Target Identifier.
2. Please produce the requested data in native electronic format (e.g., CSV, Excel). PDF exports of tabular data are unacceptable.

## DEMAND FOR PRODUCTION
You are commanded to produce the following records for the Target Identifier during the Date Range:

1. **Account Opening Documents:** Original signature cards, KYC (Know Your Customer) documentation, and Beneficial Ownership declarations.
2. **Transaction Ledgers:** Complete, unredacted transaction histories in CSV/Excel format.
3. **Wire/SWIFT Records:** Full routing data for all incoming and outgoing wire transfers, including Originator Name, Originator Account, Beneficiary Name, Beneficiary Account, and corresponding SWIFT/BIC codes.
4. **Checks & Deposits:** Front and back images of all canceled checks, deposit slips, and associated teller transaction records.

*Failure to comply with this subpoena may subject you to contempt of court proceedings.*
