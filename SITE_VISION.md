## 3. User workspaces

- Each user gets workspace(s) for their investigations — the 2026-10-30
  design: three investigations; tools used inside an investigation, never
  directly; users never see raw data or how the tools work; everything is
  filtered first; front end is a blank page with one input — the user
  tells their story however they want; the back end works it with the full
  stack; results that pass the filter compile live into a wiki-standard
  page (Wikipedia is the format standard, pages live in OsintNeoAi, not
  on Wikipedia), built in mention order, on the trinity of PAGE / GRAPH
  (Maltego) / LEDGER (immutable, first mention, also the payment rail).
  Full detail in the archive file USER-WORKSPACE-ARCHITECTURE.md.
- The user's own account is like any other user's account — no different.
  If he changes anything as admin, he figures that out later. HE signs up
  himself and puts his own story in himself (NWO RICO first).

## 4. The site's legal database — organic, like Wikipedia is built

- NOT a hosting of the absolute catalog of everything ever written.
- Laws enter as the work brings them up: inside a user's workspace, as
  they come across laws / laws are brought up in the course of their
  story, the holes get filled — that is when a law is added.
- Every law entry carries: (1) a TITLE, (2) the STATUTE, (3) a LINK to
  where it is supposed to be hosted.
- Built the way Wikipedia gets built — organically, from the stories —
  just inside this system.

## 5. Blank tools — his data is NOT stripped

- User accounts do NOT come loaded with the user's data or story.
- They get the TOOLS and PROCESSES, blank: key-holders / title-holders in
  place of the specifics. Example: a tool built to search one particular
  person in his story ships with that person's title/name replaced by a
  holder the new user fills with their own.
- His originals are NEVER stripped out or deleted. Anything taken out of
  the system to make a blank tool is done by REVISE + BACK UP + ARCHIVE —
  revision copies kept, so anything can be gone back to.
- Users get blank space to work from. He gets his own blank space, just
  like them, and fills his in himself.
