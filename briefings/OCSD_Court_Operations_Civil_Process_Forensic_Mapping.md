## ⚖️ 2. STATUTORY DUTIES & BREACHES OF THE LEVYING OFFICER

1. **Governing Authority (Cal. Code of Civ. Proc. § 715.010 et seq.):**
   * The Sheriff functions as the statutory **"Levying Officer"** charged with executing court-issued Writs of Possession for real property.
   * Under California law and the emergency mandates of **SB 91** and **AB 832 (COVID-19 Tenant Relief Act)**, the Levying Officer was statutorily prohibited from executing an eviction lockout where the tenant had submitted a timely COVID-19 Financial Distress Declaration and had a pending rental relief application.

2. **The Procedural & Evidentiary Violations:**
   * **Failure of Notice / Unposted Writ:** The armed raid on August 4, 2021 was executed via a second, unposted writ, depriving the occupant of the mandatory statutory 5-day warning period.
   * **Execution on Perjured Verification:** The writ was obtained through Shea Properties’ perjured UD-101 verification (signed May 15, 2021 falsely checking Box 7d(2) denying receipt of distress declarations).
   * **Constructive Denial of Due Process:** Shea’s opposition to the tenant's motion to vacate was intentionally mailed by developer counsel to the vacated address **16 days after the Sheriff’s lockout**, preventing court appearance.

3. **Endangerment of Vulnerable Dependents:**
   * Eight armed deputies deployed to an administrative tenancy dispute, forcibly displacing an active IHSS caregiver and a medically fragile mother undergoing congestive heart failure treatments at Hoag Memorial Hospital Presbyterian in Newport Beach.
