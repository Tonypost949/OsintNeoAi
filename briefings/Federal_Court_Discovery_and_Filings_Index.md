## ⚖️ SUMMARY OF RECOVERY STANDARDS

1. **Lawson v. PPG Architectural Finishes, Inc. (12 Cal.5th 703 - 2022):**
   * Whistleblower need only show protected disclosure was a **"contributing factor"** to adverse action.
   * Burden shifts to defendants to prove by **"clear and convincing evidence"** that adverse action would have occurred regardless.
2. **False Claims Act Relator Bounty (31 U.S.C. § 3730 & Cal. Gov. Code § 12652):**
   * Relator entitled to **15% to 30%** of total recovered government funds and statutory fines ($96M HCD fine -> $14.4M to $28.8M; $320M land recovery -> up to $96M).
3. **California Civil Code § 1942.5 & § 3294:**
   * Statutory presumption of retaliation within 180 days; Treble damages for bad-faith eviction; Punitive damages for malice and oppression ($25M–$50M).
