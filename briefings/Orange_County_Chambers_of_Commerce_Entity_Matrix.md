## ⚖️ 2. THE ANAHEIM CABAL CONSPIRACY ARCHITECTURE

As detailed in the **May 2022 FBI Criminal Complaint** (*USA v. Todd Ament*, 8:22-mj-00187):
1. **The Shadow Government:** The Anaheim Chamber of Commerce served as the operational command hub for a self-described "cabal" composed of Chamber CEO Todd Ament, political consultant Jeff Flint, and former Mayor Harry Sidhu.
2. **Covert Mock Council Meetings:** The Chamber convened closed-door sessions to script council actions, draft talking points, and direct municipal bond / land transaction approvals.
3. **Public Fund Funneling:** Chamber leadership executed fraudulent invoicing to siphon public funds under the guise of pandemic economic recovery.
