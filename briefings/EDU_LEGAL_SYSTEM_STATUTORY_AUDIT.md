## 2. STATUTORY LEGAL ANALYSIS & JURISPRUDENTIAL CITATIONS

### A. The "Triple Default" Doctrine & Void Judgments (Cal. CCP § 585 / § 473(d))
* **Factual Finding:** The docket reflects **three successive default entries**:
  1. *ROA #26 (06/29/2021):* Default Judgment by Clerk.
  2. *ROA #51 (12/22/2021):* Default Judgment by Court.
  3. *ROA #60 (02/04/2022):* Second Default Judgment by Court.
* **Governing Rule & Scholarly Precedent:**
  * *Rochin v. Pat Johnson Manufacturing Co.* (1998) 67 Cal.App.4th 1228: A judgment entered without fundamental jurisdiction is **void on its face** and may be attacked at any time.
  * *Heidary v. Yadollahi* (2002) 99 Cal.App.4th 857: Entry of a subsequent default judgment while an earlier judgment remains unvacated constitutes a fundamental jurisdictional nullity.
  * Once the court clerk entered judgment on June 29, 2021 (ROA #26), the action was **functus officio** (disposed). Subsequent ex parte default applications filed in December 2021 and January 2022 without an order vacating the prior judgment constitute misrepresentations to the tribunal under **California Rules of Professional Conduct (CRPC) Rule 3.3(a)(1)**.

### B. Shadow Service & Failure of Mandatory COVID-19 Statutory Proofs (Cal. CCP § 1179.03)
* **Factual Finding:** Summons was served via an *Application and Order to Serve Summons by Posting* (ROA #9, 06/02/2021) without demonstrating strict statutory due diligence to effect personal service.
* **Governing Rule & Scholarly Precedent:**
  * *Greene v. Lindsey* (1982) 456 U.S. 444: Posting notice on an apartment door fails Fourteenth Amendment Due Process requirements when not accompanied by robust verified mailing and genuine attempts at personal service.
  * *Cal. Code Civ. Proc. § 473.5*: A defendant who did not receive actual notice in time to defend is entitled to have default set aside as a matter of right.
  * *COVID-19 Tenant Relief Act (AB 3088 / SB 91; Cal. CCP § 1179.03)*: Required mandatory 15-day notice with specific statutory declarations; bypassing personal service to rush default violates the express public policy provisions of the Act.
