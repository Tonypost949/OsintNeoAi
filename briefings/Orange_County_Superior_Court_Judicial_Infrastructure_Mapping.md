## 💻 2. ONLINE SERVICES & COURT ACCESS ENDPOINTS

1. **Online Case Access (Civil Case Search / Register of Actions):**
   * Portal: `https://www.occourts.org/online-services/case-access`
   * Capability: Lookup by Case Number (e.g., `30-2021-01201327-CL-UD-CJC`), party name, or business entity.
2. **Civil Division & Unlawful Detainer Guidelines:**
   * Portal: `https://www.occourts.org/divisions/civil`
   * Rules governing mandatory 15-day statutory notices, COVID-19 Declarations of Financial Distress, and Emergency Eviction Moratorium Safe Harbors (SB 91 / AB 832).
3. **Tentative Rulings Portal:**
   * Portal: `https://www.occourts.org/online-services/tentative-rulings`
   * Access to departmental tentative rulings published at 2:00 PM prior to hearing dates.
4. **Court Records & Register of Actions Copy Requests (Form #L-3009):**
   * Portal: `https://www.occourts.org/online-services/court-records`
   * Certified copy purchasing for trial exhibits and Grand Jury evidentiary binders.
5. **Electronic Filing (eFiling EFSP Gateway):**
   * Portal: `https://www.occourts.org/online-services/efiling`
   * Mandatory electronic filing for civil filings and writ applications.

## ⚖️ 3. PROCEDURAL IRREGULARITIES IDENTIFIED IN CJC DOCKETS

* **Pleading Perjury under Box 7d(2):**
  * Shea Properties submitted a UD-101 complaint to the Central Justice Center clerk with a perjured verification swearing no COVID declaration was received, directly violating court filing integrity rules.
* **Denial of Due Process via Misdirected Mail:**
  * Plaintiff’s counsel deliberately mailed opposition documents to the vacated Woodbridge address 16 days after the Sheriff’s lockout, intentionally preventing tenant appearance before the CJC bench.
