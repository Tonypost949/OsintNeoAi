Information for Defendant
A defendant tenant may use this form to file a declaration of COVID-19related financial distress with the court if a plaintiff has filed
an unlawful detainer action against the defendant and asserts that a defendant did not deliver a declaration within the required 15-day
period after service of a notice demanding payment of rent or other financial obligations. (Code Civ. Proc., 5 1179.03(h).)
