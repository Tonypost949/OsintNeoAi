SAN DIEGO-Indictments were
unsealed today in San Diego federal
court charging 43 members of a
methamphetamine distribution
network tied to the Sinaloa Cartel
with federal drug trafficking and
money laundering offenses.
During the coordinated takedown
that began early this morning,
investigators executed over a dozen
search warrants and seized
approximately 80 pounds of
methamphetamine, four firearms,
and more than $100,000 in U.S.
currency. As of today at 5 p.m., 33 of
the forty-three defendants are either
in federal or state custody.
Authorities are continuing to search
for 10 defendants. Many of the
