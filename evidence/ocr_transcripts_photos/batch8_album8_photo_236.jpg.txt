Section 168le(b) of the FCRA requires that, “[wjhenever a consumer reporting
agency prepares a consumer report it shall follow reasonable procedures to assure maximum
possible accuracy of the information concerning the individual about whom the report relates." 15
U.S.C. $1681e(b) (emphasis added).
44.
Accordingly, Experian was required to follow procedures to assure maximum
possible accuracy ofthe credit reports it prepared concerning Plaintffand the Class. This includes
following procedures to prevent facially evident logical inconsistencies.

Were Experian to follow procedures to assure maximum possible accuracy of the
credit report it prepared concerning Plaintiff and the Class members, it would not be possible for

in the first place.
