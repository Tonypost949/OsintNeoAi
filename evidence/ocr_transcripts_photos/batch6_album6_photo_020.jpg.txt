The Mercy House Bed Standard
In order to maintain cleanliness and safety in this facility,all guests are expected
to follow these simple guidelines in regards to their bed areas:
All personal belongings must fit into the provided bed-side storage with lids and doors
able to close correctly. Items that can remain outside of storage include:
o Yourprogram-issuedpillow
Yourprogram-issued bedding
Your prograrn-issued laundry bag (may be filled with dirty laundry)
No morethan two pairs of shoes
During daytime hours, guests are asked to straighten their beds neatly, minimizing
clutter.During this time, the following should be completed:
o All bins and belongings should be placed on top of your bed so staff can sweep
and mop underyourbeds.
Your linens should not touch thefloor.
o If it is your laundry day,your linens should be pulled up from the mattress into
one bundle,and you should bring your linens and laundry to the laundry drop-off
As a reminder,NO OPEN FOOD maybe brought into thedorms,
The only drink allowed in the dorms is unsweetened water. Any liquids in the dorm
must be in sealable containers (no cup-and-straw drinks).
No unauthorized extension cords,electric generators, or appliances maybe used.
