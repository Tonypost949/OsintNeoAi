Guest Exits Policy
PurposeThis policy outlines how and why a guest might leave the program.
Itensuresthatexits are managed fairly and respectfully,whethera guest
finds newhousing,leaves voluntarily,ormustbe removedfor safety reasons.
The policyprovides clearsteps formanaging exits,preventing issues,and
documenting the process to keepthe shelter safe and supportive of the
needs ofboth theguest and thecommunity.
Scope: To the greatest extent possible, this policy applies to all Mercy House
emergency shelters and navigation centers, including congregate and non-
congregate facilities.
Clossary
Exit-For the sake of this policy,the term "exit"refers to the termination
"day leave,”which is when a currently-enrolled guest physically leaves
the facilityfor the day but is still expected to return to the facility by the
approved curfew.
