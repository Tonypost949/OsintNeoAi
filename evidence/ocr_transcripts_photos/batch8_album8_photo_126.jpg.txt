CIVIL CASE ACCESS
The Civil Case Information Website contains case summary and register of action
information for cases dating back to 1996for unlimited/complex civil actions and
dating back to 2005 for limited civil actions. Most documents filed on or after
January 1, 2008 may be downloaded and viewed(fees apply). Additionally,
documents purchased online can now be electronically certified (additional fees
apply). For cases and documents filed prior to these dates, contact the court
where the case was filed.
Click here for more information regarding viewing/purchasing documents as well
as a notice of sanctions for including social security numbers and financial
account information on documents filed with the court.
To access unlawful detainer / eviction cases, you must provide: the complete
name of one plaintiff, the complete name of one defendant, and the exact address
of the subject premises as provided on the complaint.
