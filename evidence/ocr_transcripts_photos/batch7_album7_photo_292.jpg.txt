Guide to Probate Code 859
By RMO Lawyers / June 10, 2020
California Probate Code s 859 is a tool in every probate litigator's
arsenal which authorizes courts to impose a monetary penalty
of "twice the value of the property recovered" against the
Wwrongdoer in certain litigation cases. Here's a quick guide.
What is California Probate Code
S 859?
California Probate Code § 859 authorizes courts to impose a
monetary penalty of "twice the value of the property recovered."
Surprisingly, this penalty also applies to the value of real
property recovered, which can lead to exorbitant penalties
reaching upwards of tens of millions of dollars. Winning such a
crushing blow against your litigation opponent may ultimately -
in the words of the iconic game Mortal Kombat - "Finish Him."
