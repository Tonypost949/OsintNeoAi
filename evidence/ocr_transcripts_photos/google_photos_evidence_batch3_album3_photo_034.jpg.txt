INTRODUCTION
This is an unlawful detainer action for non-payment of rent concerning the residential
premises located at 212 Southbrook, Irvine, CA 92604 ("Premises").Plaintiff and defendant are
parties to a lease agreement concerning the Premises. Defendant breached the agreement by
failing to pay rent.On or about March 18, 2021, in the manner required by law, Plaintiff caused
to be served upon Defendant a written Notice to Pay Rent in 15 Days or Quit in 30 Days (Covered
by CAREAS Act) With Blank Declaration of COVID-19-Related Financial Distress (Rent Due
September 1, 2020 - June 30, 2021) (the "Notice"). Defendant failed to comply with the
requirements of the Notice. Accordingly, Plaintif commenced this unlawful detainer action.
After the filing of this action, a registered California process server served copies of the
Summons, Complaint and Prejudgment Claim of Right to Possession upon the Defendant in the
manner required by law. Thereafter, the process server completed Proofs of Service attesting to
such service, which are on file with the Court. Defendant failed to timely respond to the Complaint.
Upon request by Plaintiff, the Court entered a default and default judgment in favor of Plaintiff and
against Defendantforpossession of the Premises.
On August 4, 2021, the Orange County Sheriff performed a lockout and placed plaintiff in
peaceful possessionofthePremises.
