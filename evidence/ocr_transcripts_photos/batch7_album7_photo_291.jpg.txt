(1) A description of the subject property
sufficient to provide adequate notice to any party
who may have an interest in the property. For
real property, the notice shall state the street
address or, if none, a description of the property's
location and assessor's parcel number.
(2) If the petition seeks relief pursuant to Section
859, a description of the relief sought sufficient to
provide adequate notice to the party against
whom that relief is requested.
(3) A statement advising any person interested in
the property that he or she may file a response to
the petition.
(d) The court may not shorten the time for giving
the notice of hearing under this section.
