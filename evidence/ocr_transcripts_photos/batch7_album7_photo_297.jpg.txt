The Guide to Probate Code
850 and Heggstad Petitions
By RMO Lawyers / January 6, 2020
California Probate Code 850 (commonly referred to as a
Heggstad petition) guides the legal process of avoiding lengthy
court probate administration procedures by asking a court to
order properties that should have been but were not titled in the
name of a trust. A powerful, cost-effective tool, Heggstad
petitions can save you time and money. They can also help
smoke out any potential trust contests, trust challenges, trustee
may be lurking in the wings.
