Guest Exits Policy
Introduction
A guest will be considered to have exited the program when their
enrollment has been ended due to one or more of the following:
The guest was able to secure and move into alternative housing
The guest voluntarily left the program for any reason
The guest was exited due to safety concerns and/or violating
shelterexpectations
Exit Prevention
Mercy House shelter and navigation centers operate with the common
goal of keeping people off the streets, and so staff and management
will attempt to engage guests in ways that offer guidance and
reminders any time they display behavior that may lead to an exit.
Guests will receive and be asked to complete a list of Guest
Expectations that will describe some examples of behaviors that may
result in immediate termination. In addition, they will be explained how
the Action Plan system works, and how they will be asked to enter into
an Action Plan if their behavior is noncompliant with the program's
policies. Some behavior infractions may be tolerated up to a designated
maximum number of times. Others will trigger an immediate exit.
Examples of infractions that may result in an immediate exit include,
butare not limited to:
Being in possession of illegal drugs
Possession of weapons or items that may be used as weapons
Physicalviolence towards anotherperson
Proven theft
Arson
See the Action Plan Policy for more information.
