## Deal terms

- "Service Agreement between the City of Huntington Beach and Mercy House Living Centers
  for Operations and Management of a Temporary Shelter."
- Not-to-exceed $2,528,715.13, Oct 1, 2025 – Jun 30, 2026 (9 months), General Fund.
- Option to extend up to two additional one-year terms (covers the FY2026/27 Newport MOU period).
- FY 2025-26 adopted Navigation Center budget: $3,394,800 (includes prior 3-month extension + this agreement).
- Per-bed cost $56.68/night vs regional $60–85 (Anaheim, Santa Ana, Costa Mesa).

## Procurement notes (single-bidder, twice)

- Jan 2025 RFP: only Mercy House bid; award paused on budget shortfall.
- Reissued Mar 2025 with cuts; all regional providers contacted directly; Mercy House again sole bidder.
- Cuts to fit budget: beds 174 → 164 (Jul 2025), one of two shuttles eliminated + staff,
  staffing/overhead restructure, food tied to occupancy.
- New accountability: MOPS plan (city-approved), corrective-action/payment adjustments,
  quarterly outcome + financial reporting, real-time exit/termination tracking,
  quarterly client surveys, staff parking moved off-site.

## Operations data (staff report)

- Site: 17642 Beach Boulevard. Opened 2020 with 174 beds. 990 served; 200+ housed (~1 in 5).
- 2024: 82 seniors, 18 veterans, 91% occupancy (avg 158/day, peak 168). Avg stay 4.4 months.
- 80% guests disabling condition; 61% two or more (53% MH, 43% chronic health, 42% physical disability).
- 63% chronically homeless; 10% seniors 65+. Employment 10–11%. 800+ service connections in 2024.

## Pathways permanent shelter (forward-looking)

- Jun 2024 council approved partnership with Jamboree Housing Corp to develop "Pathways,"
  a permanent shelter ON THE CITY-OWNED NAVIGATION CENTER PROPERTY.
- Jamboree pursuing funding; returns to council for final approval, then permits/construction.
- Current Navigation Center remains primary facility during development.
