### A. The $6.5M Visit Anaheim CARES Act Allocation & $1.5M Chamber Diversion

#### 1. The Rushed March 2020 Allocation
In late March 2020, during the initial emergency declarations of the COVID-19 pandemic, Mayor Harry Sidhu and his council majority rushed an emergency **$6,500,000.00 economic recovery contract** to **Visit Anaheim** (the city's contracted tourism marketing bureau).

#### 2. The Illicit $1.5M Kickback Scheme
The JL Group forensic audit uncovered that **$1,500,000.00** of these taxpayer funds was secretly diverted from Visit Anaheim to the **Anaheim Economic Development Foundation (AEDF)**—a 501(c)(3) nonprofit controlled entirely by Anaheim Chamber of Commerce CEO Todd Ament.

#### 3. The Fraudulent "Cover Story"
When city staff and internal auditors raised questions regarding the legality of transferring emergency municipal funds to a private Chamber foundation without public bidding or council disclosure, Mayor Harry Sidhu, Chamber CEO Todd Ament, and Visit Anaheim CEO Jay Burress concocted a fraudulent **"cover story."** 
* The co-conspirators agreed to falsely assert that the $1.5 million originated from Visit Anaheim's "private reserve funds" rather than the City's emergency public treasury.
* The JL Group financial trace proved this claim was mathematically and documentarily false: Visit Anaheim's private reserves were insufficient, and the funds transferred to AEDF were directly drawn from the City's $6.5M disbursement.

### C. "Anaheim First" as a Political Fig Leaf & Data-Mining Scheme

#### 1. Program Structure & Municipal Funding
Championed by Mayor Sidhu in 2019, "Anaheim First" was publicly promoted as a resident-driven, grassroots advisory board tasked with prioritizing **$250,000,000.00 in capital improvement investments** across Anaheim's neighborhoods over a 10-year period.
* The City awarded the Anaheim Chamber of Commerce a **$250,000.00/year sole-source contract** to administer the Anaheim First program.

#### 2. Forensic Audit Findings:
* The JL Group report concluded that Anaheim First was an elaborate **"political fig leaf"** and a **"thinly veiled data-mining operation."**
* Rather than empowering neighborhood residents, the Chamber used municipal funds to harvest voter contact information, survey neighborhood sentiment, and map demographic data.
* This proprietary political intelligence was then utilized by Chamber political consultants to steer municipal capital improvement projects to specific council districts, artificially boosting the electoral viability of cabal-aligned council members facing competitive reelection campaigns.

### D. Brown Act Violations & Mock Council Rehearsals

The report documented widespread, criminal disregard for California's open-meeting mandates:
1. **Serial Meetings (Cal. Gov. Code § 54952.2):** Todd Ament and Jeff Flint conducted hub-and-spoke communications with council members to build consensus on major development deals outside public scrutiny.
2. **Mock City Council Rehearsals:** Prior to major public votes—including the September 2020 stadium approval—cabal leaders held private rehearsal sessions where motions were scripted, speaking times allocated, and hostile public comments anticipated.
3. **Closed Session Appraisal Espionage:** Mayor Sidhu routinely violated Cal. Gov. Code § 54956.8 by taking confidential real estate appraisals and legal strategies from closed sessions and leaking them directly to buyer representatives.

### E. California Public Records Act Evasion & Evidence Spoliation

1. **Systemic CPRA Evasion (Cal. Gov. Code § 7920 et seq.):**
   * Cabal members and cooperating city officials systematically conducted official municipal business on personal Gmail accounts, personal cell phones, and encrypted messaging platforms (Signal, WhatsApp) to avoid disclosure under the California Public Records Act.
2. **Spoliation & Intentional Deletion:**
   * The audit documented extensive, intentional destruction of public records. When federal subpoenas and CPRA requests were served, Mayor Sidhu, Todd Ament, and allied operatives deleted hundreds of text messages and email chains regarding stadium negotiations, Chamber contracts, and campaign financing.
