### B. Statutory Charges & 4 Felony Counts
Defendant Todd Ament entered a binding plea agreement of guilty to the following four felony counts:

1. **Count 1: Wire Fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343)**
   * **Scheme:** Devising and executing a scheme to defraud the Anaheim Chamber of Commerce and a private client organization by diverting chamber revenues, sponsorship funds, and client consulting fees into his private shell entity, **TA Group LLC**.
   * **The Big Bear Residence Fraud:** Ament diverted **$225,000** in Chamber funds through TA Group LLC accounts to fund the purchase of a personal secondary vacation home located in Big Bear Lake, California.
2. **Count 2: False Statements to a Financial Institution (18 U.S.C. § 1014)**
   * **Scheme:** Commercial mortgage fraud and submission of fraudulent loan applications to a federally insured mortgage lender.
   * **Overt Act:** In connection with financing the Big Bear residence, Ament submitted falsified bank statements, forged liquid asset verifications, and fabricated documentation purporting to show that $205,000 in diverted funds represented legitimate earned consulting income rather than unearned Chamber advances.
3. **Count 3 & Count 4: Subscribing to False Federal Income Tax Returns (26 U.S.C. § 7206(1))**
   * **Scheme:** Filing materially false, perjured IRS Form 1040 federal income tax returns.
   * **Overt Act:** For tax years 2017, 2018, and 2019, Ament knowingly and willfully underreported his taxable income, concealing hundreds of thousands of dollars in illicit income diverted from the Chamber of Commerce, client retainers, and TA Group LLC distributions.

### C. Role in "The Cabal" & Shadow Governance Operations
* **Ring Leader of Shadow Government:** FBI wiretap disclosures and forensic audit findings established that Todd Ament operated as the primary orchestrator of a tight-knit, unelected "cabal" controlling City of Anaheim public policy.
* **The Monterey Chamber Retreat:** Ament organized private, closed-door policy retreats (e.g., December 2020 Monterey Conference) attended by Mayor Harry Sidhu, political consultant Jeff Flint (FSB Core Strategies), and select city executives where municipal legislative priorities, tax subsidies, and council voting strategies were pre-arranged outside public Brown Act transparency.
* **Public Funds Diversion:** Ament facilitated the improper steering of over **$1,500,000** in municipal economic recovery and COVID-19 relief contracts to the Anaheim Chamber and affiliated nonprofit conduits (Visit Anaheim / Anaheim Tourism Improvement District).

* **Presiding Judge:** Hon. Cormac J. Carney, U.S. District Judge
* **Prosecuting Authority:** United States Attorney's Office, Central District of California
* **Investigative Agency:** Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) — Public Corruption Squad
* **Defense Counsel:** Jennifer L. Keller / Jesse A. Gessin (Keller/Anderle LLP)

### B. Statutory Charge & Criminal Information
1. **Count 1: Attempted Wire Fraud (18 U.S.C. §§ 1343, 1349)**
   * **Scheme to Defraud Honest Services:** Scheme to deprive the City of Irvine, its City Council, and its residents of the intangible right to the honest services of its public officials through bribery, kickbacks, and corruption.
   * **The Irvine Commercial Cannabis Bribery Scheme:**
     * In 2018, Rafiei agreed to solicit and accept bribe payments totaling tens of thousands of dollars (**$20,000 to $25,000+**) from commercial cannabis business entrepreneurs (who were cooperating with or posing as clients in federal undercover operations).
     * In exchange for the illicit payments, Rafiei promised to direct and influence specific members of the Irvine City Council to introduce, champion, and enact municipal commercial cannabis licensing and zoning ordinances favorable to the paying clients.
     * Rafiei instructed the cannabis operators to structure and disguise the corrupt payments as legal retainer fees, political consulting contracts, and conduit campaign donations to avoid campaign finance reporting scrutiny.

### C. Federal Law Enforcement Cooperation
* Following an FBI undercover sting operation in 2019, Rafiei agreed to cooperate with federal law enforcement authorities.
* Between 2019 and 2021, Rafiei served as a registered cooperating witness, wearing covert audio/video recording equipment and participating in consensual intercepts to record conversations with Orange County elected officials, political consultants, and municipal figures regarding public corruption and influence-peddling networks.
* The government formally filed the Felony Information in January 2023 pursuant to the negotiated Rule 11 plea agreement reflecting her proffer and historical cooperation.
