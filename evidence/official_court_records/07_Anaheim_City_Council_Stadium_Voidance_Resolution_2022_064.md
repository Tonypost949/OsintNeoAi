### 1. Serial Meetings & Hub-and-Spoke Evasion (§ 54952.2(b)(1))
California Government Code § 54952.2(b)(1) provides:
> *"A majority of the members of a legislative body shall not, outside a meeting authorized by this chapter, use a series of communications of any kind, directly or through intermediaries, to discuss, deliberate, or take action on any item of business that is within the subject matter jurisdiction of the legislative body."*

The FBI wiretaps and independent forensic audits revealed that Chamber CEO Todd Ament and lobbyist Jeff Flint functioned as private intermediaries, conducting sequential one-on-one meetings with Council Members to coordinate voting blocs and draft pre-approved questions to create the illusion of independent council scrutiny.

### 2. Secret "Mock City Council" Rehearsals
FBI Special Agent Brian Adkins documented that on September 21, 2020—prior to the September 29 public hearing approving the stadium sale—Sidhu, Ament, and Flint convened a clandestine rehearsal meeting. Participants role-played potential council debates, scripted Mayor Sidhu's public remarks, and formulated talking points designed to marginalize dissenting council members and housing advocates.

### 3. Abuse of Closed Session Safe Harbors (§ 54956.8)
Under Cal. Gov. Code § 54956.8, a city council may hold closed sessions with its designated real property negotiators *solely* to give instructions regarding the price and terms of payment. Mayor Sidhu abused this narrow exception by obtaining confidential city-commissioned appraisals (which valued the 150 acres between $320M and $500M) and immediately relaying the appraisal metrics and negotiation bottom-lines to Angels representatives, directly compromising the City's fiduciary position.

## 6. DISENTANGLEMENT & POST-TERMINATION STATUS

1. **City Attorney Notice (May 25, 2022):** City Attorney Robert Fabela formally delivered the termination declaration to SRB Management Co. LLC, stating that the agreement was unenforceable as a matter of law due to the tainted procurement process and violations of the Brown Act and California common law principles of contract integrity.
2. **SRB Concurrence & Escrow Release (May 27, 2022):** SRB Management Co. LLC delivered formal written concurrence, executing mutual escrow cancellation instructions. The $50,000,000.00 deposit was returned to SRB, and all lis pendens or encumbrances against the 150-acre parcel were released.
3. **Preservation of 150-Acre Public Asset:** By enacting Resolution No. 2022-064, the City of Anaheim preserved full municipal title to the 150-acre Platinum Triangle property, preventing the discounted transfer of public land and establishing the legal foundation for the subsequent JL Group independent forensic investigation.
