#### 3.1.2 Minute-by-Minute Chronological Incident Reconstruction

* **14:16 hrs (2:16 PM):** Hamilton Police Dispatch broadcasts a priority call for service at `1456 Cedar Lane` regarding an irate male (identified by calling party Karen Steward as Dean Innocenzi) throwing objects around his residence, operating a circular saw and hammer, and making repeated suicidal statements following the death of his dog.
* **14:17 hrs (2:17 PM):** Patrol Unit 710 (Officer Timothy Donovan #484) arrives on scene, followed immediately by Patrol Unit 701 (Officers Kevin Perkins #506 and Richard McLaughlin #536). Officers proceed to the rear yard of 1456 Cedar Lane, encountering Innocenzi visibly agitated and working with tools.
* **14:18 hrs (2:18 PM):** Officer Perkins interviews Innocenzi regarding his statements. Innocenzi states he is attempting to construct a wooden coffin for his deceased dog and states verbatim:
  > *"Why would I want to live right now? My dog's the one I love."*
* **14:19 hrs (2:19 PM):** Officer John Murphy (#531, Unit 712) arrives on scene. Based on the subject's overt suicidal statements, Officer Murphy advises Innocenzi that officers are required under New Jersey law to transport him to the Crisis Center at Capital Health Regional Medical Center for psychiatric evaluation.
* **14:20 hrs (2:20 PM):** Innocenzi refuses to comply, stating he will not go to crisis, turns abruptly, and sprints toward the back door of the residence. Officer Donovan positions himself on the rear porch to block the entrance. Innocenzi rushes Officer Donovan, grabs Donovan by the ballistic vest and left shoulder, and attempts to violently throw Donovan out of his path.
* **14:20:30 hrs:** Officer Perkins grabs Innocenzi by the head while Officer Donovan grapples with his upper torso. The officers force Innocenzi to the ground adjacent to the rear porch. Innocenzi falls directly on top of Officer Perkins amidst a large pile of broken, cut lumber containing exposed nails.
* **14:21 hrs (2:21 PM):** Sergeant Timothy Wilkes (#443) and Officer Michael Durand (#457) arrive and join the physical struggle. Innocenzi actively resists lawful commands to place his hands behind his back. Due to subject resistance and build, officers utilize **two pairs of handcuffs** connected together to secure his arms behind his back, which are then double-locked by Officer Durand and Officer McLaughlin.
* **14:23 hrs (2:23 PM):** Officers assist Innocenzi to his feet. Officers observe minor lacerations on the left and right sides of Innocenzi's face caused by contact with the lumber debris and nails during the struggle. No police officers sustain physical injury.
* **14:25 hrs (2:25 PM):** Innocenzi is placed into the rear prisoner compartment of Patrol Unit 701 and transported against his will to the **Crisis Center at Capital Health Regional Medical Center (Helene Fuld Hospital)**, located at 750 Brunswick Avenue, Trenton, NJ 08638.
* **Post-Incident Administration:**
  * Officer Timothy Donovan executes **Complaint Summons No. `1103-S-2019-002671`** charging Dean Innocenzi with a violation of **N.J.S.A. 2C:29-1a (Obstructing Administration of Law)**. The summons is mailed to the subject address at `1456 Cedar Lane, Hamilton, NJ 08610`.
  * BlueTeam use-of-force incident reports are logged by Officer Donovan and Officer Perkins.
  * Officer Perkins and Officer Donovan document that their body-worn cameras (BWCs) were recording during initial contact but were deactivated/turned off during the physical body impact and grapple on the ground.
