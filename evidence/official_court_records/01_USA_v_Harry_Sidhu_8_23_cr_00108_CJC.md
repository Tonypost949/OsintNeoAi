## 2. STATUTORY CHARGES & 4-COUNT FELONY INFORMATION

Defendant Harish "Harry" Sidhu was formally charged with and agreed to plead guilty to a four-count Felony Information:

Count One:    18 U.S.C. § 1343          Wire Fraud (Honest Services / Commercial Fraud)

Count Four:   18 U.S.C. § 1001(a)(2)    False Statements to Federal Agency (FAA)
================================================================================

### Count 1: Wire Fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343)
* **Statutory Description:** Scheme to defraud the City of Anaheim, its City Council, and the citizens of Anaheim of their right to honest services, and to fraudulently obtain property and proprietary negotiating intelligence.
* **Overt Factual Acts:**
  * While serving as Mayor of Anaheim and functioning as a designated member of the City's negotiating team for the sale of the 150-acre Angel Stadium property, Sidhu covertly transmitted confidential, proprietary City information to representatives and consultants of the Los Angeles Angels baseball team (SRB Management LLC / Arte Moreno).
  * In September 2019, Sidhu unlawfully provided a non-public, internal commercial land appraisal valuing the stadium property to Angels consultants weeks before the appraisal was disclosed to City Council members or the public.
  * Sidhu transmitted closed-session City Council negotiation parameters, internal price floors, and tactical advice via text messages and private electronic mail to assist the Angels in acquiring the stadium property for $320,000,000 (subsequently reduced to $150,000,000 cash net of community benefit credits).
  * Sidhu engaged in this covert scheme with the corrupt expectation and intent of soliciting a **$1,000,000 political campaign contribution** from Angels representatives to fund his 2022 mayoral re-election campaign.

### Count 2: Obstruction of Justice (18 U.S.C. § 1519)
* **Statutory Description:** Knowingly altering, destroying, mutilating, concealing, and falsifying records, documents, and tangible objects with the intent to impede, obstruct, and influence the proper administration of a matter within the jurisdiction of the FBI and a federal grand jury.
* **Overt Factual Acts:**
  * Beginning in or around 2020, upon learning of inquiries and investigations regarding the Angel Stadium land transaction, Sidhu knowingly deleted and purged incriminating electronic communications from his personal and official cellular devices and email accounts.
  * Sidhu destroyed written records, email threads, and text messages documenting his secret communications with Chamber of Commerce CEO Todd Ament and political consultant Jeff Flint (FSB Core Strategies), specifically targeting records reflecting the scripted "mock city council meetings" and confidential information leaks.

### Count 3: False Statements to Federal Law Enforcement (18 U.S.C. § 1001(a)(2))
* **Statutory Description:** Knowingly and willfully making materially false, fictitious, and fraudulent statements and representations in a matter within the jurisdiction of the executive branch of the Government of the United States.
* **Overt Factual Acts:**
  * On September 16, 2022, during a formal voluntary interview with Special Agents of the Federal Bureau of Investigation, Sidhu falsely stated:
    1. That he never provided confidential, non-public negotiation data, appraisal figures, or closed-session information to representatives of the Angels.
    2. That he did not expect, request, or anticipate receiving financial campaign contributions or political funding from Angels affiliates in connection with the stadium land transaction.
  * Both statements were materially false, as Sidhu had repeatedly leaked confidential data and had been wiretapped explicitly detailing his $1,000,000 campaign solicitation plan.

### Count 4: False Statements to the Federal Aviation Administration (18 U.S.C. § 1001(a)(2))
* **Statutory Description:** Materially false statements submitted to the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) regarding aircraft registration and taxation.
* **Overt Factual Acts:**
  * In or around 2020–2021, Sidhu purchased a Robinson R44 helicopter for approximately $158,875.
  * To evade California state sales and use taxes totaling approximately **$15,887.50**, Sidhu submitted fraudulent registration and purchase documentation to the FAA falsely certifying that the helicopter was purchased and based at an out-of-state address in Arizona, despite taking delivery of and operating the aircraft in California.

## 3. FBI WIRETAP SEARCH WARRANT AFFIDAVIT (SA BRIAN ADKINS)
**Docket Reference:** Case No. `8:22-mj-00185` (Central District of California)  
**Affiant:** Special Agent Brian Adkins, Federal Bureau of Investigation  
**Unsealing Date:** May 16, 2022 (36 Pages)  

### A. The $1,000,000 Quid Pro Quo Intercept
On **December 14, 2021**, Mayor Sidhu met privately with Anaheim Chamber of Commerce CEO Todd Ament. Unbeknownst to Sidhu, the meeting was recorded by federal investigators. During the meeting, Sidhu detailed his plan to demand a $1,000,000 political campaign contribution from an Angels team consultant/representative immediately following the closure of the stadium transaction:

> *"I am going to ask him for $1 million... I'll say, 'You know what? I'm going to need $1 million to get reelected... I have to raise it.'... We'll have to get it from Angels people."*
> — **Harry Sidhu, recorded December 14, 2021 (Affidavit ¶ 42)**

### B. Scripted "Mock City Council" Rehearsals
The affidavit revealed that prior to official City Council hearings on the stadium sale, Sidhu, Chamber CEO Todd Ament, and political consultant Jeff Flint (FSB Core Strategies) conducted private, scripted "mock council meetings":
1. **Choreographed Questioning:** The cabal scripted specific questions for compliant council members and pre-formulated defensive talking points to neutralize public opposition and bypass municipal scrutiny.
2. **Exclusion of Non-Aligned Officials:** City staff and dissenting council members were deliberately excluded from genuine negotiations and kept uninformed regarding side agreements.
3. **Leaked Appraisals:** Sidhu leaked a confidential City appraisal (valuing the stadium land at up to $500M under alternative development scenarios) directly to Angels consultants to ensure the Angels could lock in the discounted $320M headline price.

### C. Grand Jury Witness Tampering & Evidence Destruction
* Intercepted communications confirmed Sidhu met with a witness subpoenaed by the Orange County Grand Jury.
* Sidhu coached the witness to provide false, exculpatory testimony and instructed the witness to delete electronic communication trails and text messages linking Sidhu to secret policy meetings.

## 4. PLEA AGREEMENT TERMS & SENTENCING PROVISIONS

Under the Rule 11 Plea Agreement executed on August 16, 2023:
1. **Guilty Plea:** Sidhu entered a binding plea of guilty to all four felony counts.
2. **Statutory Maximums:**
   * Count 1 (Wire Fraud): Up to 20 years federal imprisonment.
   * Count 2 (Obstruction of Justice): Up to 20 years federal imprisonment.
   * Count 3 (False Statements to FBI): Up to 5 years federal imprisonment.
   * Count 4 (False Statements to FAA): Up to 5 years federal imprisonment.
   * *Cumulative Statutory Maximum:* **54 Years Imprisonment**, a $1,000,000 fine, and 3 years supervised release.
3. **Restitution & Tax Penalties:** Mandatory restitution to the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration for unpaid helicopter taxes ($15,887.50) plus applicable penalties and interest.
4. **Waiver of Appeal:** Sidhu waived all constitutional and statutory rights to appeal the conviction and sentence, except under narrow jurisdictional exceptions.
