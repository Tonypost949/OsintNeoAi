# "HB's Navigation Center Reduces Senior Homelessness" — Fountain Valley Living, Jan 29, 2026

Source: https://www.fvlm.com/living/hbs-navigation-center-reduces-senior-homelessness
By: Vanessa Siguenza. Captured: 2026-10-05. Status at capture: NOVEL.

## Key facts

- 82 seniors served through HB Navigation Center in 2024 (city staff figure).
- 619 shelter referrals by city social workers (some repeat); 326 external referrals to
  family/DV shelters; police facilitated 145 shelter referrals.
- Jessica Kelley (city homeless + behavioral health manager): Center is "so much more than
  a place to sleep" — meals, hygiene, 1-on-1 housing workers. Mobile medical van, SUD/
  therapeutic programs, employment help, vet services for pet owners.
- Pelican Harbor Senior PSH: 18431 Beach Blvd, 4-story, 42 affordable units, case manager +
  services coordinator on-site. Partnership: Huntington Beach + County + Jamboree (2024).
- Huntington Beach Cares (volunteer initiative) supports seniors, veterans, families.
- Regional spillover: Fountain Valley residents benefit via county partnership.

## Why it matters

- Independent corroboration of Jamboree + city/county permanent-supportive-housing pattern
  (same developer as planned "Pathways" permanent shelter on the Navigation Center parcel).
- Outcome metrics (619 referrals, 82 seniors) supplement the Legistar 25-467 staff report.
- Kelley is the city's named program lead (also co-author of 25-467 staff report).
