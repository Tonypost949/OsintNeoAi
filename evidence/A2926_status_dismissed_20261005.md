## Finding

The active-petitions table (as of September 1, 2026) lists ONLY A-2940, A-2945, A-2946,
A-2948, A-2951, A-2953. A-2926 (Knabb petition challenging the 8/21/2020 OCHCA 20IC002

## Why absent = dismissed

The page states verbatim: "Active water quality petitions that were received by the State
Water Board prior to June 3, 2026 are not listed below because they were dismissed by
operation of law effective on the 91st day after receipt, unless (1) the State Water Board
granted review or (2) the petitioner placed the petition into abeyance" (23 CCR 2050.5(e)).
