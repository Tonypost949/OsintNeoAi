## E. Imperial County in 1942 — was it evacuated? DOCUMENTED: YES

Imperial County was inside **Military Area No. 1** and was evacuated by exclusion order, to Poston:

- **Civilian Exclusion Order No. 71** (Western Defense Command, May 14, 1942): "all persons of Japanese ancestry, both alien and nonalien, be excluded from that portion of **Military Area No. 1** described as follows: All of that portion of the County of Imperial, State of California…" — report to the Civil Control Station at Japanese Methodist Church, 336 North Seventh Street, **Brawley**, May 15–16, 1942; exclusion effective **12 noon, Thursday, May 21, 1942**. Primary text (USF Gleeson Library digital collections): https://digitalcollections.usfca.edu/digital/api/collection/p15129coll10/id/2158/download
- **Civilian Exclusion Order No. 72** (May 14, 1942): the remainder of Imperial County, including **El Centro** ("together with all parcels of Imperial County not heretofore covered by exclusion orders"), same May 21, 1942 deadline; Civil Control Station at Japanese Buddhist Church, 455 Commercial Street, El Centro. Poster text (CSUDH digital collections): http://digitalcollections.archives.csudh.edu/digital/api/collection/p16855coll4/id/5908/download
- Imperial Valley Historical Society (Pioneers Museum) history: "The Imperial Valley's Japanese population was interned at the **Poston** Relocation Center near Parker, Arizona" — https://nrm.dfg.ca.gov/FileHandler.ashx?DocumentID=8662

**Consequence for this search:** a child born in Imperial County in 1934 and living there in 1942 WOULD be expected to appear in the WRA file, at Poston — and a name + birth-year + Imperial-County-address row for Mitsuru Yamada is present (§B.1). The absence of Hisako (b.1923) from the file cannot be explained by geography; if she was in Imperial or Orange County in 1942 she should appear under some name form — under "Hisako Yamada" she does not appear at all, and the only Hisako-bearing Yamada records are the 1927 FAR-Ancestry near matches (§D.3), with Ireizō still unsearched (§C).
