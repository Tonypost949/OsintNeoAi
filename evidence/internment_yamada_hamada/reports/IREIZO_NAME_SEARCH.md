# Ireizō Name Search — Yamada + Hamada

Run live in the browser, 2026-11-06 01:11–01:15 PST, at https://ireizo.org/ (the site's "SEARCH BY NAME" panel; https://ireizo.org/search itself returns the site's 404 page). Continuation of ~/workspace/task_system/YAMADA_NAME_SEARCH.md — the index that could not be searched in that pass. Ireizō is the searchable archive of the Ireichō (Japanese American National Museum / USC Shinso Ito Center): 126,333 persons, 75 incarceration sites per its own description. Each entry shows name, birth year, and camps/facilities only. No entry below is claimed as an identity match beyond name + birth-year agreement.

**Search-reliability note (documented by the run itself):** Ireizō's full-name search misses entries whose stored name contains a middle name. The surname browses (Yamada 749 entries; Hamada 237 entries) were therefore scanned in full, and the results below include entries the name search missed.

## Yamada (surname browse: 749 entries)

1. **Mitsuru Yamada** — 6 entries. Two born 1934: **MITSURU YAMADA, 1934 — POSTON and TULE LAKE** (the camp pair matches the NARA Family 21270 trajectory exactly: Poston entry 1942, transfer to Tule Lake 1943-10-07); and MITSURU THOMAS YAMADA, 1934 — MANZANAR, SACRAMENTO ASSEMBLY CENTER, TULE LAKE (the UNVERIFIED second candidate of the earlier report). Others: 1890, 1907, 1925, 1940.
2. **Shigeru Yamada** — TWO entries born 1928: **SHIGERU YAMADA, 1928 — POSTON** (only); and **SHIGERU WILLIAM YAMADA, 1928 — POSTON and TULE LAKE**. Consistency note (stated as consistency, not determination): in the NARA/FAR record, Family 21270's Shigeru (FAR other-name "William") was transferred Poston → Tule Lake with his whole family on 1943-10-07, while the Santa Ana Family 24045 Shigeru has no Tule Lake transfer in the records pulled. Ireizō's two entries split the same way — the "William" entry carries Tule Lake, the plain entry does not.
3. **Hisako Yamada (b. March 1923)** — **NOT FOUND in Ireizō.** The only Hisako is MABLE HISAKO YAMADA, 1927 (Heart Mountain, Jerome, Pinedale Assembly Center, Tule Lake). The full 749-entry Yamada scan confirms the 1923 group contains no Hisako. Combined with the earlier pass: Hisako Yamada b.1923 is absent from the NARA Internee Data File, the Densho Names Registry, and Ireizō — all three indexes now searched.

## Hamada (surname browse: 237 entries)

4. **YASABURO HARRY HAMADA, 1881 — JEROME, SANTA ANITA ASSEMBLY CENTER, TULE LAKE** (missed by the name search; found in the surname browse). Matches the walkthefarm family head and the Form 26 Jerome family.
5. **SHIKI HAMADA, 1901 — JEROME, SANTA ANITA ASSEMBLY CENTER, TULE LAKE.**
6. **BEN HAMADA, 1921 — JEROME, SANTA ANITA ASSEMBLY CENTER, TULE LAKE.**
7. **Namiye (b.1923)** — no "Namiye Hamada" exists in the database. The 1923 Namiye on the Hamada family's exact camp path is **NAMIYE OKADA, 1923 — JEROME, SANTA ANITA ASSEMBLY CENTER, TULE LAKE**. Stated as a lead: the walkthefarm piece documents Namiye Hamada marrying Manabu Okada; an entry under the married name on the family's camp path is consistent with that, and is not a name match.
8. **GEORGE TSUYOSHI HAMADA, 1925 — JEROME, ROHWER, SANTA ANITA ASSEMBLY CENTER** (the Rohwer leg differs from the Jerome → Tule Lake path of the others; transfers between camps were common; reported as shown).
9. **HISAKO BETTY HAMADA, 1922 — PARKER DAM RECEPTION CENTER (AZ), POSTON** (missed by the name search, which returned only a Hisako Hamada b.1906, Gila River; found in the surname browse). "Betty" is the same other-name the FAR Poston roster gives for the Hisako Hamada of Santa Ana, Family 10106 — the two records agree on name + other-name + birth year + Poston.
