## PULL 1 — Original Tule Lake Final Accountability Roster (RG 210), Family 21270

### DOCUMENTED — the original roster series, its roll, and a freely readable scan

1. **The series.** "Final Accountability Rosters of Evacuees, 1944–1946,"
   National Archives Identifier (NAID) **1055789**, Record Group 210
   (Records of the War Relocation Authority). NARA microfilm publication
   **M1865**. Source: NARA, "List of National Archives microfilm publications
   and original records relating to Japanese Americans in World War II"
   (PDF): https://Www.archives.gov/files/research/aapi/wwii-incarceration-records-list.5.2024.pdf
   (the series is also listed on NARA's Japanese-American incarceration
   research page: https://www.archives.gov/research/aapi/ww2/incarceration —
   which separately lists the Tule Lake–specific series "Lists of Evacuees at
   the Tule Lake Segregation Center," NAID 1541663, and "Evacuee Case Files,"
   NAID 891305.)
2. **The Tule Lake roll.** Tule Lake's roster is **Roll 10** of M1865;
   FamilySearch digitized it as DGS folder **101044949** (613 images) in its
   collection "United States, Final Accountability Rosters of Evacuees at
   Relocation Centers, 1944-1946" (collection 2729264):
   https://www.familysearch.org/search/collection/2729264
3. **A complete original scan is freely readable on the Internet Archive:**
   item **densho-nr-far-tulelake**, "Final Accountability Roster of the Tule
   Lake Relocation Center, March 1946, Volumes I and II" (Densho's scan of
   the same roster): https://archive.org/details/densho-nr-far-tulelake
   Files include the full PDF (~677 MB), an OCR text PDF (~107 MB), djvu.txt
   (~916 KB) and djvu.xml with per-word coordinates.
4. **Why the original matters:** the Densho Names Registry Tule Lake records
   used in the earlier pulls are Ancestry's transcription of these rosters
   and carry **no destination field** (Densho's dataset documentation:
   https://ddr.densho.org/names/). The original ledger pages record, per
   person, departure type, departure date, and destination — the fields this
   pull was after.

### COULD NOT EXTRACT — the nine Family 21270 records from the original

The original scan was located and its full OCR text layer was downloaded and
machine-searched (Internet Archive djvu.txt and djvu.xml, 615 scanned pages).
**No per-person departure type / date / destination could be extracted
reliably**, for measured reasons:

- The djvu text layer loses the ledger's row alignment: the roster is a
  tabular, ditto-marked document, and OCR reading order does not preserve
  which destination belongs to which person.
- The surname "YAMADA" survives OCR in only **2 literal occurrences** in the
  entire text layer. Coordinate reconstruction (djvu.xml word positions)
  places legible YAMADA surname entries only on scanned pages **610–611** —
  the tail of a family-number-ordered section (families in the ~2938–2941
  range, surnames TOKUHARA … YAMADA) — not in the main alphabetical sequence,
  and not anchored to Family 21270.
- In the main alphabetical Y-run (scanned pages ~562–575: YAMAGUCHI,
  YAMAMOTO, YAMANAKA …), the YAMADA surname itself is OCR-garbled
  (renderings such as "YANVABE," "YAMAST"), while the family's given names
  (Yutaka, Yoshiye, Hiroshi …) recur across many unrelated families and
  cannot be tied to the surname or to each other from the text layer.
- The Internet Archive's own full-text inside-search for this item returned
  an error ("No hOCR or Abbyy file present"), so IA search-inside could not
  be used either.

**Consequences for the two flagged cases:**
- **(a) Mitsuru Yamada, b.1934 (Family 21270I):** the original record was
  **NOT EXTRACTED**. The inconsistency in the Ancestry/Densho transcription
  (a Tule Lake record under "Mitsuru Thomas," entry 1942-06-22, departure
  1943-09-11 — the other candidate's name/dates) therefore **stands
  unresolved**. NOT FOUND in the original by any route usable here.
- **(b) Haruye Yamada, b.1919 (Family 21270A):** **NOT FOUND** in the
  original by text-layer search either; the text layer cannot prove absence
  (see garbling above), so this is **NOT EXTRACTED**, not a negative finding.
- The remaining seven (Yutaka 1891, Shizuko 1900, Takashi 1921, Yoshiye
  1923, Hiroshi 1925, Shigeru 1928, Fusaye 1931): same status — the
  transcription's Tule Lake departure dates already on file in
  AREA_AND_TULELAKE_RESULTS.md stand (entered 1943-10-09; departures
  1945-10-22, 1945-11-08, 1946-03-14), but **departure type and destination
  remain undocumented**.

### Exact settling routes (named, not used — with why)

1. **Visual reading of the roster images** — M1865 Roll 10 via FamilySearch
