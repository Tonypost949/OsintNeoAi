STATE
ENT QE ISSUE:
In 1999, the City established a policy clarifying the level of environmental review for historic
structures listed in the General Plan.  Basically, any demolition of a listed historic structure was
subject to environmental review under the California Environmental Quality Act ( CEQA).
Additionally, any construction work on a historic structure was required to comply with the
Secretary of the Interior' s Standards for the Treatment of Historic Properties in order to be
exempt
from environmental
review.  However, based on recent CEQA research, it has been
determined that a ministerial demolition permit or alterations to a historic resource are not
subject
to CEQA.  In other words, a request to demolish a historic structure is currently exempt
under CEQA and no further environmental
review is necessary.
It is important that prior to demolition of historic structures, an environmental
analysis be
completed
to determine the impact
of the proposed demolition
on the historic
structure.  In order
to do this, the city needs to adopt
an ordinance.  I recommend that the City Council direct staff
to prepare an ordinance codifying the prior city policy.
RECOMMENDED
ACTION:
v
1.    
Direct
staff to prepare
an ordinance
codifying
the prior city policy which
required
environmental
review ( CEQA) of any proposal to a demolish
historic structure or alter a
historic structure when not in compliance with the Secretary of the Interior's Standards
for the Treatment of Historic Properties.
