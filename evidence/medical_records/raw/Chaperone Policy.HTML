Chaperones for Sensitive Exams and Procedures
At UCI Health, we provide personalized and compassionate care to our patients. We understand that some exams and procedures are more sensitive than others. For your safety and comfort, UCI Health offers chaperones to accompany you during intimate and sensitive exams or procedures to ensure that your care is provided in a considerate and compassionate manner.
 
How do I request a chaperone?
 
To request a chaperone for your exam or procedure, or if you have any questions or concerns, please speak to one of our healthcare team members.
 
What is an intimate and sensitive exam or procedure?
 
Exams of the rectum, breast or genitalia are considered intimate and sensitive. However other examinations or procedures may be considered intimate, therefore a chaperone may be provided at a patient’s request.
 
What does the chaperone do?
 
The chaperone oversees the exam or procedure, remaining in close proximity as care is being provided to fully observe and support the patient as needed. The presence of a chaperone during an exam or procedure is meant to safeguard a patient’s and provider’s safety, dignity and confidentiality.
 
Who can act as a chaperone?
 
For patients 15 years and older, the chaperone is an authorized UCI Health employee. At a patient’s request, family members may be present during an exam or procedure, but may not be used as a chaperone for patients.
 
For pediatric patients 14 years and younger, the chaperone is an authorized UCI Health employee. If requested, parents and/or guardians may serve as a chaperone for their child.
 
Can I decline to have a chaperone present during my exam or procedure?
 
Patients may decline the presence of a chaperone during their exam or procedure at any time during their care. In the event a provider is uncomfortable proceeding with an examination or procedure without a chaperone, care may be postponed or rescheduled for another time.
 
Can I request a gender-specific chaperone?
 
Each patient has the right to request a gender-specific chaperone. Please let your healthcare team know if you have a preference. We will do our best to accommodate your request. After a discussion with your healthcare team, you may choose to postpone or reschedule your visit for another time if we are unable to meet your preference.
