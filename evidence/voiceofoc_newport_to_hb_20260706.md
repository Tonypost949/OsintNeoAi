# Newport Beach Shifts Homeless Shelter Service to Huntington Beach

Source: https://voiceofoc.org/2026/07/newport-beach-shifts-homeless-shelter-service-to-huntington-beach/
By: Lauren Contreras, Voice of OC. Published: Jul 6, 2026. Captured: 2026-10-05.
Status at capture: NOVEL — not in repo vault at time of hunt.

## Key facts

- Newport Beach moved shelter services from Costa Mesa Bridge Shelter to Huntington Beach Navigation Center.
- One-year agreement: 10 exclusive beds for Newport Beach homeless + 20 hours/week social services support.
- 7 of 10 beds reserved for males, rest for females.
- Cost: $44,100/month, $529,200/year. Per-bed rate $132/night (vs Bridge Shelter $163).
- One-time $9,000 to convert an older HB vehicle for the part-time social worker.
- Adopted 2026-27 budget had $1,698,282 for Costa Mesa Bridge Shelter; switch saves ~$1.2M.
- Newport terminated its social services provider (People Assisting the Homeless); services now part-time at HB shelter.
- Newport PIT count: 8 unsheltered people; credited to overhauled anti-camping ordinance (tent bans, bench/bike-rack lodging bans).
- Renewal option: two additional one-year terms.
- HB City Council approved the partnership June 16, 2026 (Legistar ID 8067681, GUID 9A0A8F29-041A-4849-ACF4-73B966AD9807).
- Newport approved June 23, 2026. Prior deal: 25 exclusive Bridge Shelter beds (Dec 2020–Jun 30, 2026), cut to 20 in Dec 2025.
- Councilmember Noah Blum (Newport): "They've stepped up in a way that ... Costa Mesa never could."
- Councilmember Robyn Grant (Newport): 90% drop in street sleeping; savings toward permanent supportive housing.
- Related: Irvine partnered with Costa Mesa Bridge House March 2026 at $163/bed/night, expired June 30, 2026.
