## 3. ⚖️ LEGAL & EVIDENTIARY FRAMEWORK (STATUTORY AUTHORITIES & EXAMPLES)

In accordance with legal, investigative, and data analysis standards, all repository records maintain a strict distinction between objective public data points, structural observations, investigative leads, and adjudicated legal findings.

### A. Statutory & Evidentiary Authorities

1. **Federal Rules of Evidence (FRE Rule 803(8)) — Public Records Exception**
   * *Authority:* Fed. R. Evid. 803(8); *Beech Aircraft Corp. v. Rainey*, 488 U.S. 153 (1988).
   * *Application:* Recorded deeds, Secretary of State corporate filings, SBA loan disclosures, and state environmental records (e.g., GeoTracker data) are objective public records admitted as factual matters recorded by public offices.

2. **Judicial Notice of Public Records & Government Databases (FRE Rule 201)**
   * *Authority:* Fed. R. Evid. 201; *Khoja v. Orexigen Therapeutics, Inc.*, 899 F.3d 988 (9th Cir. 2018); *Denius v. Dunlap*, 330 F.3d 919 (7th Cir. 2003).
   * *Application:* Facts contained within official government databases (SOS filings, SBA registries, county recorder indices) are subject to judicial notice because their accuracy cannot reasonably be questioned.

3. **Distinction Between Structural Associations and Adjudicated Liability**
   * *Authority:* *Twombly v. Bell Atl. Corp.*, 550 U.S. 544 (2007); *Ashcroft v. Iqbal*, 556 U.S. 662 (2009).
   * *Application:* Parallel conduct, common addresses, shared trustees, or adjacent property parcels constitute structural observations. Assertions of illegal conduct, fraud, or conspiracy require adjudicated legal findings (e.g., court judgments, formal administrative enforcement orders, or indictments).

4. **Public Record Privilege & Neutral Reporting Doctrine**
   * *Authority:* Cal. Civ. Code § 47(d); *Khawar v. Globe Internat., Inc.*, 19 Cal. 4th 254 (1998).
   * *Application:* Fair, true, and neutral reporting of public records and official government filings is protected and must refrain from asserting unadjudicated conclusions as established legal guilt.
