- **18 U.S.C. § 1951 (The Hobbs Act — Extortion)**:
  Obtaining property or rights from another with consent induced by wrongful use of actual or threatened force, violence, or fear (including economic fear and manufactured litigation).
- **18 U.S.C. § 1512(b), (c) (Witness & Informant Tampering / Intimidation)**:
  Engaging in misleading conduct, harassment, or threats to hinder, delay, or prevent the communication of information relating to federal statutory/civil rights violations.

  Knowingly taking any harmful action, including damaging economic livelihood or shelter status, in retaliation for providing truthful information to law enforcement.
- **18 U.S.C. § 1962(c), (d) (Civil RICO Enterprise & Conspiracy)**:
  Conducting an enterprise through a pattern of racketeering activity, where predicate acts include extortion (Hobbs Act), mail fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1341), and wire fraud (18 U.S.C. § 1343).
- **42 U.S.C. § 1985(2), (3) (Conspiracy to Deprive Civil Rights & Obstruct Justice)**:
  Two or more persons conspiring to deter, by force, intimidation, or threat, any party or witness from attending court or testifying freely, or to injure them for having testified or filed.

### B. CALIFORNIA STATE STATUTES
- **California Penal Code § 518 (Extortion Defined)**:
  Obtaining property or consideration from another by wrongful use of force or fear.
- **California Penal Code § 519 (Fear Constituting Extortion)**:
  Inducing fear by threats to do an unlawful injury to the person or property of the individual.
- **California Penal Code § 523 (Extortion by Threatening Letter / Electronic Communication)**:
  Sending or delivering threatening communications with intent to extort money, property, or legal forbearance.
- **California Civil Code § 52.1 (The Tom Bane Civil Rights Act)**:
  Interfering by threat, intimidation, or coercion with the exercise or enjoyment by any individual of rights secured by the Constitution or laws of the United States and California.
- **California Labor Code § 1102.5(b) (Whistleblower Retaliation)**:
  Prohibits retaliating against an individual for disclosing information to a government agency where the individual has reasonable cause to believe the information discloses a violation of state or federal statute.
