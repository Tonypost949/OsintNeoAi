# Formal Notice of Litigation Hold & Duty to Preserve All Evidence
**Case:** *Knabb v. City of Huntington Beach et al.*  
**Docket:** 8:26-cv-00348-JWH-ADS (U.S. District Court, Central District of California)

---

**TO:**  
Office of the City Attorney, City of Huntington Beach  
City Council & Department Heads (Public Works, Utilities, Code Enforcement)  
2000 Main Street, Huntington Beach, CA 92648  

### 1. Pending Federal Litigation
An active citizen enforcement suit under the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act (RCRA), 42 U.S.C. § 6972, is pending in the U.S. District Court for the Central District of California.

### 2. Mandatory Duty to Preserve
Pursuant to the Federal Rules of Civil Procedure (including Rules 26 and 37) and governing federal common law, the City and all associated departments, officers, employees, contractors, and agents have an affirmative, non-delegable duty to preserve all documents, data, and tangible items potentially relevant to this action.

### 3. Scope of Preservation
* **Electronically Stored Information (ESI):** All emails, SMS/text messages, internal memos, GIS/mapping data, and deleted file recovery caches.
* **Environmental Data:** All continuous monitoring logs, manifests, discharge reports, inspection records, soil/water analytical lab results, and chain-of-custody forms.
* **Physical Site Status:** All physical barriers, waste containment areas, drainage infrastructure, and contaminated media must not be altered, concealed, or disposed of without prior written notice and opportunity for inspection/split sampling.

### 4. Spoliation Warning
Any alteration, automatic deletion (auto-purge schedules), modification, or destruction of relevant evidence will be grounds for sanctions under Fed. R. Civ. P. 37(e), including mandatory adverse inference instructions, striking of defenses, and monetary penalties.
