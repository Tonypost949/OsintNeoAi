TEST IT AND LOOK AT IT — BEFORE YOU ZIP IT
Perplexity's piece was delivered untested and it showed: its nodes all rendered on top of each other and half its controls were dead. Do not repeat that. Before delivering:
- Run / open the finished piece and click EVERY control and interactive element. Confirm each one visibly works.
- Look at the actual rendered result — a screenshot or a real render, not just the code — at desktop width and at phone width (~390px). Judge it as a viewer.
- Quality bar, all of it mandatory: nothing overlaps or bunches into one blob at the default view; every label is legible and separated; every control does something on every axis it claims; no dead buttons; no console errors; it looks like a finished piece you would sign.
- If anything fails that bar, fix it and look again. Then zip.
- If your platform lets you attach an image, attach a screenshot of the final render with your reply as proof you looked.
