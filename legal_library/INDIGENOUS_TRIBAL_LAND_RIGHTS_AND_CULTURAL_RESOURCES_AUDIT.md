### II. ANCESTRAL TRIBAL TERRITORIES & SACRED SITES IN ORANGE COUNTY

#### 1. The Tongva (Gabrielino) & Acjachemen (Juaneño) Nations
* **Tongva Ancestral Lands (*Tovaangar*):** Spanning the entire Los Angeles Basin, coastal Orange County (Huntington Beach, Newport Beach, Seal Beach, Bolsa Chica), the San Gabriel Valley, and the southern Channel Islands (Santa Catalina, San Clemente, San Nicolas).
* **Acjachemen Ancestral Lands:** Encompassing southern Orange County (San Juan Capistrano, Dana Point, San Clemente, Aliso Creek basin), northern San Diego County, and western Riverside County.
* **The Shared Coastal Estuary Corridor:** The Huntington Beach / Bolsa Chica / Santa Ana River delta served as a critical trade nexus, seasonal village site, and ceremonial burial area for over **9,000+ years of continuous habitation**.

#### 2. Key Documented Tribal Cultural Sites
* **Bolsa Chica Archaeological Complex (CA-ORA-83 — The Cogged Stone Site):** One of the oldest continuously occupied coastal sites in North America (dating back 8,000–9,000 years BP). Known for unique carved cogged stones and significant sacred burial grounds.
* **Santa Ana River & Talbert Marsh Corridor:** Historical gathering zones, freshwater artesian springs (*puuvungna* linkages), and traditional medicinal plant cultivation corridors.
* **Magnolia / Ascon Superfund Adjacent Parcels:** Industrial development that bypassed tribal consultation risks disturbing subsurface archaeological deposits and sanctified cultural layers.

### III. CALIFORNIA STATUTORY TRIBAL PROTECTIONS (CEQA & AB 52)

#### 1. California Assembly Bill 52 (AB 52, Gatto 2014)
* **Codified Law:** California Public Resources Code §§ 21074, 21080.3.1, 21080.3.2, 21082.3, 21084.2.
* **Key Mandate:** AB 52 created a new environmental category under the California Environmental Quality Act (CEQA): **Tribal Cultural Resources (TCRs)**.
* **Government-to-Government Consultation Requirement:**
  1. Lead agencies (e.g., City Councils, Planning Commissions, Coastal Commission) **must provide formal written notification** within 14 days of deciding to undertake a project to all California Native American tribes traditionally and culturally affiliated with the geographic area.
  2. The tribe has **30 days** to request consultation.
  3. The consultation must occur **prior to the release of a Negative Declaration, Mitigated Negative Declaration, or Environmental Impact Report (EIR)**.
  4. Failure to conduct good-faith government-to-government consultation renders the environmental review legally defective under CEQA.

#### 2. Native American Heritage Commission (NAHC)
* **Sacred Lands File (SLF):** The state's confidential database of sanctified Native American sites, cemeteries, and ceremonial locations.
* **Most Likely Descendant (MLD) Protocol (Cal. Pub. Res. Code § 5097.98 & Cal. Health & Safety Code § 7050.5):**
  * When human remains are discovered during any construction or grading activity, **all work within 100 feet must immediately cease**.
  * The County Coroner must be notified within 24 hours.
  * If the Coroner determines the remains are Native American, the NAHC must be contacted within 24 hours.
  * The NAHC assigns an official **Most Likely Descendant (MLD)** from the culturally affiliated tribe to inspect the site and make binding recommendations for preservation in place or respectful reburial.
