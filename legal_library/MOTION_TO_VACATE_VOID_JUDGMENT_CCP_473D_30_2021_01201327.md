### MEMORANDUM OF POINTS AND AUTHORITIES

#### I. INTRODUCTION & SUMMARY OF GROUNDS
Under California Code of Civil Procedure § 473(d), a court may, on motion of either party or on its own motion, set aside any void judgment or order at any time. A judgment is void on the face of the record where the court lacked subject-matter jurisdiction, where statutory conditions precedent were fraudulently circumvented, or where mandatory statutory stays were unlawfully evaded.

Here, the record establishes that:
1. **Perjured Statutory Verification (Form UD-101):** On May 15, 2021, Plaintiff executed a false and perjured verification on Judicial Council Form UD-101, falsely asserting compliance with statutory COVID-19 relief declarations (falsely checking Box 7d(2)) to evade mandatory statutory clerk stays.
2. **Pending Emergency Rental Assistance Moratorium:** At the time of filing (May 18, 2021) and the subsequent entries of default, Defendant had an active, pending Emergency Rental Assistance Program (ERAP) application under Cal. CCP § 1179.03, which statutorily deprived the clerk and Court of authority to enter default.
3. **The Triple Default Record Anomaly:** The certified Register of Actions (civilwebshopping.occourts.org) reflects irreconcilable, conflicting entries of default at **ROA #26, #51, and #60**, demonstrating profound clerical, procedural, and jurisdictional irregularity rendering the judgment void *ab initio*.

#### III. LEGAL ARGUMENT

##### A. Standard of Review Under Cal. CCP § 473(d)
California Code of Civil Procedure § 473(d) provides: *"The court may, upon motion of the injured party, or its own motion, correct clerical mistakes in its judgment or orders as entered, so as to conform to the judgment or order directed, and may, on motion of either party after notice to the other party, set aside any void judgment or order."* 
A judgment void on the record may be attacked collaterally or directly at any time, without limitation (*Falahati v. Kondo* (2005) 127 Cal.App.4th 823, 830; *Rochin v. Pat Johnson Manufacturing Co.* (1998) 67 Cal.App.4th 1228, 1239).

##### B. Strict Compliance with Unlawful Detainer Statutes Is Jurisdictional
Because Unlawful Detainer is a summary statutory proceeding, strict adherence to all procedural and statutory mandates is jurisdictional (*Borsuk v. Appellate Division* (2015) 242 Cal.App.4th 607, 613). The filing of a false UD-101 verification constitutes extrinsic fraud that strips the Court of jurisdiction to enter default.

##### C. The Multi-Entry Default Anomaly (ROA #26, #51, #60) Invalidates Subsequent Execution
The issuance of multiple entries of default against a single defendant on identical claims creates a structural record defect. Once a clerk improperly enters default without statutory authority during a mandatory ERAP stay, all subsequent writs and enforcement proceedings are legal nullities.
