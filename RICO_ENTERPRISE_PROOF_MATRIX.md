## Scoring
Use a simple 0–3 score for each row:
- **0 — Unsubstantiated:** allegation or lead only.
- **1 — Corroborated lead:** one credible source, but material alternatives remain.
- **2 — Supported inference:** multiple independent sources converge.
- **3 — Strong proof candidate:** authenticated evidence plus competent witness or expert foundation, with no substantial unexplained contradiction.

*Do not average the scores into a single “enterprise score.” A high aggregate score should not conceal a zero on attribution, intent, or causation.*

---

## Minimum Corroboration Rule
For each major coordination proposition, seek:
1. One **primary source**—original record, custodian-produced file, transaction record, or contemporaneous communication.
2. One **independent corroborator**—different custodian, witness, system, or record series.
3. One **tested alternative explanation** documented in the case file.
4. One **foundation path** identifying who can authenticate or explain the evidence.
