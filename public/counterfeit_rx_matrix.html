Interstate-5 Pacific Rim Corridor (COR-01)

Transit Route: Tijuana / Otay Mesa POE → San Diego → Orange County (Irvine/Santa Ana) → Los Angeles → Central Valley → Sacramento → Portland → Seattle.
Primary Contraband & Seizures: Bulk industrial presses of M-30 Fentanyl and Meth-pressed Adderall. Over 35M+ pills intercepted along the California I-5 corridor since 2021.

Transcontinental I-10 Vector (COR-02)

Transit Route: Nogales / El Paso / Laredo POEs → Phoenix → Tucson → Las Cruces → San Antonio → Houston → New Orleans → Atlanta.
Modus Operandi: Commercial tractor-trailer hydraulic traps, commercial freight rail, and multi-kilo bulk pill caches distributed to southeastern cartel hubs.

Midwest Rust Belt Vector (COR-03)

Transit Route: Chicago Intermodal Rail Hub → Indianapolis → Detroit → Columbus → Cleveland → Pittsburgh.
Emerging Threat: Concentrated emergence of synthetic Nitazenes (Isotonitazene / Protonitazene) yielding extreme mortality in opioid-tolerant populations.

International Mail & Darknet Hubs (COR-04)

Transit Route: JFK, ORD, LAX, and MIA International Mail Facilities → USPS Express / FedEx / UPS Domestic Distribution.
Contraband Profile: Counterfeit Ozempic/Mounjaro pens, darknet Bromazolam powder drops, and industrial rotary pill press die punches imported under fraudulent customs manifests.
