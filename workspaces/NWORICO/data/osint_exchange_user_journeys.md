# Dual-Ledger User Journeys & Real-World Mechanics

## 1. The Contributor: Marcus (Data Miner)
*   **Role:** OSINT and Public Records Investigator.
*   **Action:** Finds an anomaly (e.g., shell entity siphoning grants). Uploads source PDFs and analysis tagged with `[Tax-Funded]` and `[OSINT]`.
*   **Validation:** System checks file integrity (hashing, timestamps). Peer validators review metadata.
*   **Reward:** Block is created. Marcus earns newly minted OSINT and TFT fractions based on value tier.
*   **Bounty Trigger:** A journalist breaks a story using the data. The institutional bounty hits the escrow.
*   **Payout:** The smart contract reads Marcus's UTXO data lineage and automatically sends his weighted fractional share of the payout in stablecoin (USDC) to his wallet.
*   **Liquidity:** Marcus connects to a decentralized, whitelisted liquidity pool to swap some earned tokens for fiat.

## 2. The Outside Investor: Elena (Secondary Market)
*   **Role:** Retail crypto investor seeking exposure to the decentralized intelligence network.
*   **Compliance:** Completes lightweight KYC to verify her wallet for the whitelisted trading pools (RWA backing compliance).
*   **Action:** Uses USDC to buy OSINT and TFT on the open secondary market.
*   **Staking:** Stakes a portion of her holdings in security pools to earn passive protocol yield (generated from API query fees charged to corporate investigators).
*   **Value Appreciation:** As major crimes are solved and institutional users buy access, Elena's holdings increase in value based on fundamental utility and real-world cash-flow injections, not just speculative hype.
